Chess Chronicles · Perspective Engine

Setup Tool — Configure your collaborative book, then export a student-facing scaffold.

1 Paste PGN

2 Story Details

3 Define the Kings (MacGuffin / NPC / Goal)

Kings are not assigned to students. They represent what each side is fighting for — a captured leader, a sacred artifact, a homeland, etc.

4 Assign Characters

Assign each piece to a student. Name their character, describe them, and add character-specific motifs. Toggle pawn companions to extend thin stories.

5 Export

Generate a standalone HTML file that students use as their story scaffold reference. Filterable by piece, with all character names, themes, and motifs baked in.
