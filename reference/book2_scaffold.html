Victor Hernandez — Split Arc Scaffold

The Migration
Victor begins paired with Leah on the f1 Bishop. When Leah is incapacitated at M4 (bxc4), Victor doesn't fall with her — he migrates to Ben's unit (c1 Bishop) and continues the fight from there.
Phase 1: With Leah (M1–M4)
• The analytical backbone to Leah's emotional fire
• Deploys early with Leah (Bc4, M2) — aggressive positioning
• Witnesses Leah's incapacitation at M4 — the moment everything changes
• Must choose: fall with her or carry on
Phase 2: With Ben (M4–End)
• Arrives carrying grief for Leah's fall
• Channels grief into analytical support for Ben's patient defensive posture
• Helps Ben determine the timing for their single critical move at M22 (Be3)
• Survives the endgame — but carries the weight of watching Leah fall AND watching from the sidelines while Ahdia delivers the final blow alone
Writing Prompt
Victor's migration is the quiet tragedy of this game. The systems thinker watches his first partner fall, adapts, finds a new unit, contributes to one crucial moment — and then watches from the margins as someone else finishes the fight. He did everything right. He adapted. He survived. And he still feels like he didn't do enough.

The both/and: Victor is both the person who saved Ben's unit by providing analysis AND the person who couldn't save Leah. Both are true. Neither cancels the other.
