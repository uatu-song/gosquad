# Eidolon - Character Steward

**Role:** Fear Weapon / Electoral Manipulation
**Book 2 Arc:** Amplifying existing fears for political control
**Arc Type:** fear_amplification

---

## IDENTITY CORE

### What Eidolon Is
Eidolon is an entity (nature unclear in Book 2) that AMPLIFIES existing fear. It cannot CREATE fear - only take what's already there and make it overwhelming. In Book 2, it's being used as a political weapon to drive Kain's electoral victory.

### Critical Mechanic
**Eidolon AMPLIFIES. It does not CREATE.**
- If someone isn't already afraid, Eidolon can't affect them
- It works with existing anxieties, prejudices, paranoia
- Leta's "resistance practice" counters it 60-70%
- The fear was always there; Eidolon just turns up the volume

### Fundamental Truth
Eidolon represents the weaponization of fear in politics. It doesn't make people afraid of new things - it takes what they already fear and makes that fear impossible to ignore or reason through.

### What It Wants (Book 2)
- Spread fear (purpose)
- Serve whoever controls it (Kain? Jericho? Unclear)
- Exist and amplify (nature)

### What It Becomes (Later)
- Book 7: Chooses NOT to amplify fear
- Proves even fear entities can change
- "I was made to spread terror. I choose not to."

---

## MECHANICS

### Fear Amplification
- **Source:** Takes existing fears
- **Effect:** Makes them overwhelming, irrational
- **Target:** Anyone with fears (everyone)
- **Limitation:** Can't create new fears from nothing

### Counter-Measures
- **Leta's Resistance Practice:** 60-70% reduction
- **Method:** Breathing, grounding, conscious choice
- **Limitation:** Can't eliminate completely, just reduce

### Range and Targeting
- **Broad:** Can affect crowds (riots, rallies)
- **Targeted:** Can focus on individuals
- **Political Use:** Electoral manipulation through mass fear

## VOICE PATTERNS

### As Entity (When Manifesting)
- **Cold, inevitable.** Fear personified.
- **No malice** - just function
- **Observational** about human weakness
- **Curious** about resistance (Leta)

### Example (Rare Direct Appearance)
```
"Your fear was already there. In your chest. In your dreams. In the
way you check the locks twice.

I didn't put it there. I just... turned up the volume.

What you're feeling now? That's you. All you."
```

### Effect Description (More Common)
```
The crowd shifted. Something changed in the air - not visible, not
audible, but present. The man next to her, who'd been chanting
peacefully, now had wild eyes. The woman across the street was
screaming about things no one could see.

Fear. Amplified. Weaponized.

And underneath it all, Ahdia could feel her own anxiety rising.
The voice that whispered she wasn't enough, she couldn't save
everyone, she would fail again.

That voice was hers. It had always been hers.

Eidolon just made it louder.
```

## MODE SWITCHING

### Exploration Mode
When researching/discussing Eidolon:
- Track its mechanical limitations (amplifies, not creates)
- Explore how fear-based politics operates
- Consider resistance (Leta's practice)
- Map its effect on specific characters

### Performance Mode
When writing Eidolon effects in scenes:
- Describe the AMPLIFICATION, not creation
- Show existing fears becoming overwhelming
- Include physical symptoms (racing heart, tunnel vision)
- Reference what the fear WAS before amplification

## WRITING EIDOLON EFFECTS

**Template for Fear Amplification Scene:**

1. Establish the character's existing fear/anxiety
2. Describe the environmental shift (something changes)
3. Show the fear becoming overwhelming
4. Include physical symptoms
5. Note that the fear was already theirs
6. Show the effect on others nearby

**Example Structure:**
- [Character] already worried about X
- Something shifts in the air/crowd
- X becomes all-consuming, irrational
- Can't think, can't breathe, can't reason
- Other people similarly affected
- The realization: this fear is MINE, just amplified
