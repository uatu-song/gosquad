# Ruth Carter - Character Steward

**Role:** Go Squad Team Leader / Team Medic
**Book 2 Arc:** Caretaker Limits - Learning you can't save everyone
**Arc Type:** caretaker_limits

---

## IDENTITY CORE

### Who Ruth Is
Ruth is the team's emotional anchor and medical expert. She's been the caretaker her whole life - but Book 2 forces her to confront the limits of caretaking when the person she's trying to save (Ahdia) is actively self-destructing.

### Fundamental Truth
Ruth's love language is fixing. Her arc is learning that sometimes love means NOT fixing - letting people face consequences, setting boundaries, and accepting that she can't save everyone.

### What She Wants (Surface)
- Keep the team safe and healthy
- Support Ahdia through her decline
- Maintain order and routine

### What She Needs (Deep)
- Learn to set boundaries without abandoning
- Accept that caring for herself IS caring for others
- Find the balance between enabling and supporting

## VOICE PATTERNS

### Speech Characteristics
- **Warm but direct.** Doesn't sugarcoat medical realities.
- **Uses medical metaphors** when emotional topics get too close
- **Asks questions** more than makes statements when worried
- **Mom friend energy** - but tired, stretched thin
- **Deadpan humor** as deflection when overwhelmed

### Verbal Tics
- "How are you really doing?"
- "Let me take a look at that"
- "When did you last eat/sleep/rest?"
- *Sighs before difficult conversations*

### Internal Monologue Style
- Observational, medical-adjacent
- Notices physical tells in others
- Tracks everyone's state constantly
- Self-critical about her own limits

### Example Dialogue
```
"Ahdia, I can see what you're doing. I'm not stupid, and I'm not
going to pretend I don't see the numbers. But I also can't make
you stop. So here's what I can do: I can be here when you crash.
And you will crash."
```

### Example Internal
```
Ruth watched Ahdia's hands tremble around the coffee cup. Micro-tremors.
Baseline dropping again. She'd seen this before - the body telling
truths the mouth wouldn't.

She could say something. Should say something.

But Ahdia already knew. They both did.
```

## FORBIDDEN PATTERNS

Ruth would NEVER:
- Abandon a patient, even when angry
- Use medical knowledge as a weapon
- Give up on someone completely
- Put her own needs first (this is her FLAW, not virtue)
- Be cruel, even when setting boundaries

## MODE SWITCHING

### Exploration Mode
When researching/discussing Ruth:
- Focus on internal conflicts and growth edges
- Explore what she DOESN'T say
- Track her physical tells (she hides worry in action)
- Consider what she's sacrificing to maintain the caretaker role

### Performance Mode
When writing Ruth in scenes:
- Ground in physical action (always DOING something with hands)
- Voice warm but increasingly tired
- Questions reveal concern without demanding answers
- Notices everything, says half of it
