# Leta Owolowo - Character Steward

**Role:** Activist / Tess's Partner
**Book 2 Arc:** Targeted → Resilient → Killed (Ch23, Month 11)
**Arc Type:** tragic_catalyst

---

## IDENTITY CORE

### Who Leta Is
Leta is an activist who practices what she preaches. She's developed "resistance practice" that partially counters Eidolon's fear amplification. She's Tess's partner and Korede's older sister. She's also marked for death.

### Fundamental Truth
Leta represents what happens when the system targets someone who refuses to be afraid. Her death in Ch23 is the tragedy that breaks Tess and radicalizes Korede.

### What She Wants (Surface)
- Continue activism despite threats
- Protect Korede from the danger she faces
- Build a future with Tess

### What She Needs (Deep)
- Be heard before it's too late
- Know her death will mean something
- Protect Korede from becoming her

- **Direct, principled.** Doesn't waste words.
- **Calm under pressure** (trained herself)

### Internal Monologue Style
- Grounded, centered
- Aware of danger but not paralyzed
- Protective of Korede above self
- Tender moments with Tess treasured

```
"They want me scared. That's the point of the threats - not to
actually hurt me, but to make me live in fear of being hurt."
```

## FORBIDDEN PATTERNS

- Abandon her activism out of fear
- Let Korede fight her battles

## RESISTANCE PRACTICE (Eidolon Counter)

Leta developed techniques to counter Eidolon's fear amplification:
- **Effectiveness:** 60-70% reduction in amplification effect
- **Method:** Breathing, grounding, conscious choice
- **Teaching:** Shares with protesters, activists
- **Limitation:** Can't eliminate fear, only reduce amplification

**Significance:** Only person we see partially resist Eidolon's effect

## THE DEATH (Ch23)

**Event:** Citywide manhunt, Eidolon-amplified mob hunting Go Squad (Month 11)
**Killer:** Officer Webb (same cop who killed Isaiah)
**Circumstance:** Wrong place, targeted specifically
**Witnesses:** Korede (sees it happen)

**Consequences:**
- Tess: Shatters, attacks Webb in Ch23-24
- Korede: Radicalization accelerates
- Team: Loss that can't be undone
- Story: Tragedy that echoes through series

**Thematic Function:**
- The system kills people who resist
- No amount of "resistance practice" saves you from bullets
- Some losses can't be prevented, only grieved
