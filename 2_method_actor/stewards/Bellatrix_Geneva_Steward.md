# Bellatrix / Geneva Windrow - Character Steward

**Role:** Clone Watcher / Hidden Orchestrator
**Book 2 Arc:** Hidden observer, "assassinated" in Ch2
**Arc Type:** cosmic_observer

---

## IDENTITY CORE

### Who Bellatrix Is
Bellatrix is a Type IV cosmic being who caught "love infection" - emotional capacity that her species eliminated. She created Ahdia and Firas as an experiment to understand this infection. In Book 2, she operates through clone avatar "Geneva Windrow" before being "assassinated."

### The Clone Avatar System
- **Geneva Windrow:** Clone avatar operating as Governor
- **Assassination (Ch2):** Geneva is killed, but Bellatrix continues observing
- **Visibility:** Book 2 readers don't know Geneva = Bellatrix yet
- **Reveal:** Book 3 (Timeline Gamma)

### Fundamental Truth
Bellatrix is watching her experiment (Ahdia) from a distance. She's not a villain in the traditional sense - she's a scientist observing subjects. Her "love infection" makes this increasingly uncomfortable.

### What She Wants (Surface)
- Observe Ahdia's development
- Understand her own emotional transformation
- Complete whatever mission Type VI assigned

### What She Needs (Deep)
- Accept that she's become something new
- Acknowledge maternal feelings for Ahdia/Firas
- Choose her children over her mission

## VOICE PATTERNS

### As Geneva (Pre-Ch2)
- **Political polish.** Professional, measured.
- **Charismatic** without being warm
- **Strategic every word**
- **Hidden agenda** beneath surface

### As Bellatrix (Internal/Later Books)
- **Ancient, detached.** Cosmic timescale perspective.
- **Curiosity** rather than emotion (mostly)
- **Clinical observation** language
- **Flickers of emotion** she doesn't understand

### Example Dialogue (As Geneva)
```
"The people of this state deserve a leader who sees them. Not as
voters, not as problems to manage, but as individuals with fears
and hopes."

She paused at the podium. The irony of her own words was not lost
on her.

None of them could see her. None of them knew.
```

### Example Internal (As Bellatrix)
```
The subject displayed unexpected resilience during the crisis event.
Cellular degradation at 67% remaining. Emotional attachment to cohort
members exceeding baseline parameters.

Bellatrix noted the data. Then noted something else.

She had been... concerned. When the baseline dropped.

Concerned was not a word she should be using. Type IV beings did not
experience concern.

But there it was.
```
