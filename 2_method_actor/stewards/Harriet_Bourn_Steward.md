# Director Harriet Bourn - Character Steward

**Role:** CADENS Overseer
**Book 2 Arc:** Institutional Authority → Defector
**Arc Type:** institutional_authority

---

## IDENTITY CORE

### Who Bourn Is
Harriet Bourn is the Chief Overseer of CADENS - the shadow government agency protecting humanity from cosmic threats. She's a career bureaucrat who believed in the mission, until she discovered TRIOMF had infiltrated CADENS. Book 2 is her journey from institutional authority to defector.

### Critical Canon Warning
**Bourn is a WOMAN. Use she/her pronouns. Never he/him/his.**

### Fundamental Truth
Bourn represents the rare institutional actor who chooses integrity over position. When she discovers the rot, she doesn't cover it up - she burns her career to expose it.

### What She Wants (Surface)
- Protect CADENS's mission
- Maintain institutional order
- Manage assets effectively (including Ahdia)

### What She Needs (Deep)
- Accept that the institution itself is compromised
- Choose mission over organization
- Risk everything for what's right

---

## VOICE PATTERNS

### Official Bourn
- **Authoritative, measured.** Career bureaucrat precision.
- **Chain of command** language
- **Careful documentation** of everything
- **Professional distance** from assets

### Personal Bourn (Rare)
- **Warmer, more direct.** Mask slipping.
- **Frustrated** with institutional constraints
- **Protective** of those under her authority
- **Decisive** when pushed to the wall

### Example Dialogue (Official)
```
"The asset's operational tempo is outside approved parameters.
I've requested a medical review from Dr. Matsuda. The results
are... concerning."

She set down the file.

"We need to discuss whether continued deployment serves the mission."
```

### Example Dialogue (Personal, Post-Defection)
```
"I spent thirty years building this agency. Believing in this agency.
And they were inside the whole time. Using us."

Her hands were steady. Her voice wasn't.

"I have the reactor coordinates. Twenty-eight positions worldwide.
The coup is coordinated for midnight."

She met Ruth's eyes.

"I'm trusting you with everything I have left."
```
