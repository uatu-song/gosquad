# Ben Bukowski (Night Knight) - Character Steward

**Role:** Go Squad Evidence Builder
**Book 2 Arc:** Institutional Faith Collapse ("What am I conserving?")
**Arc Type:** institutional_faith_collapse

---

## IDENTITY CORE

### Who Ben Is
Ben is the team's conservative conscience - a former military man who believed in institutions, evidence, and playing by the rules. Book 2 systematically destroys that faith when perfect evidence fails to change anything.

### Fundamental Truth
Ben's worldview depends on evidence mattering. When Eidolon reframes perfect evidence as "proof of conspiracy," his entire framework collapses. The question "What am I conserving?" isn't rhetorical - it's existential.

### What He Wants (Surface)
- Build airtight case against Kain
- Prove the system works when given good evidence
- Protect his team through proper channels

### What He Needs (Deep)
- Accept that evidence alone isn't enough
- Find new reasons to fight beyond institutional faith
- Integrate cynicism without losing hope entirely

## VOICE PATTERNS

### Speech Characteristics
- **Precise, methodical.** Military background shows.
- **Evidence-focused** - cites sources, tracks data
- **Patient explainer** - breaks down complexity
- **Dry humor** - rarely jokes but deadpan when he does
- **Formal when stressed** - retreats to procedure

### Verbal Tics
- "The evidence shows..."
- "Here's what we know for certain"
- "That's not how this works"
- *Clears throat before difficult statements*

### Internal Monologue Style
- Analytical, list-making
- Military tactical assessment
- Increasingly desperate pattern-matching
- Faith struggling against evidence

### Example Dialogue
```
"I spent six months building this case. Every document verified.
Every source triple-checked. Every claim backed by physical evidence.
And you're telling me it doesn't matter because people are afraid?"

He sat back. "What the hell am I conserving?"
```

### Example Internal
```
The polling numbers didn't lie. Kain up twelve points.

Ben had given the media everything. The offshore accounts. The
falsified safety reports. The body count. Everything documented,
everything verifiable.

None of it mattered.

He'd spent his whole life believing evidence was the foundation.
Now he was watching the foundation crack.
```
