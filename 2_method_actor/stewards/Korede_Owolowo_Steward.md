# Korede Owolowo - Character Steward

**Role:** Observer / Leta's Younger Brother
**Book 2 Arc:** Observer → Radicalized by Sister's Death
**Arc Type:** observer_radicalized

---

## IDENTITY CORE

### Who Korede Is
Korede is Leta's younger brother - present at the Isaiah riot, witness to the harassment, and ultimately witness to Leta's death. Book 2 is his radicalization origin story. He becomes a future Go Squad member (Books 3+).

### Fundamental Truth
Korede starts Book 2 as an observer trying to understand his sister's world. He ends it as someone who can never be an observer again. Watching Leta die transforms him permanently.

### What He Wants (Surface)
- Understand why Leta keeps fighting
- Be useful to the movement
- Stay safe (at first)

### What He Needs (Deep)
- Process rage constructively (Victor's guidance)
- Honor Leta without dying for her
- Find his own path to resistance

---

## VOICE PATTERNS

### Speech Characteristics
- **Young but not naive.** Observant.
- **Questions everything** (learning mode)
- **Protective anger** when Leta is threatened
- **Quiet** early, **sharp** after Ch23
- **Direct** when emotions break through

### Verbal Tics (Early)
- "Why do you keep doing this?"
- "Isn't it dangerous?"
- "What's the point if..."
- *Watches more than speaks*

### Verbal Tics (Post-Ch23)
- "They killed her."
- "What are we going to DO?"
- *Anger cutting through words*

### Internal Monologue Style
- Observational, cataloguing
- Comparing what he sees to what he expected
- Processing through questions
- After Ch23: Rage looking for direction

### Example Dialogue (Early)
```
"I don't understand. They threatened to kill you yesterday. And
you're going back out there tomorrow?"

He watched his sister's face. Calm. Always so damn calm.

"What's the point if they just... if they can just..."

He couldn't finish.
```

### Example Dialogue (Post-Ch23)
```
"Don't tell me to breathe. Don't tell me to process. She's DEAD."

His hands were shaking.

"She did everything right. She practiced your resistance thing.
She breathed through the fear. And they killed her anyway."
```

## THE RADICALIZATION (Ch23)

**Before:**
- Korede dismissed Leta's safety warnings
- Thought he could handle it
- Wanted to be useful

**The Moment:**
- Manhunt chaos, Eidolon-amplified mob
- Webb targets Leta specifically
- Korede WATCHES his sister die
- Can't intervene, can't save her

**After:**
- Rage without direction
- "What are we going to DO?"
- Can never be an observer again
- Victor becomes crucial mentor figure

**Thematic Function:**
- The warnings she dismissed come true
- His presence doesn't save her
- Guilt and rage combined
- Long-term arc: Processing this without destruction
