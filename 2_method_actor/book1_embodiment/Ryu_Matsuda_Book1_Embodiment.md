> **What this is.** The psyche you perform Ryu from. His defining structural fact: a triplet made entirely of other people's pieces. Don't look for his arc in what he *does*; find it in where he *stands.* He is proximity without possession.

## Who he is in Book 1 (one breath)

A brilliant, motor-mouthed temporal-physics scientist who runs CADENS's FAERIS operations and becomes Ahdia's handler: the guy in the chair who built the system that watches her, monitors the cells that are killing her, and falls in love with her without ever once saying it. His role is proximity without possession: always beside her, helping, watching her dying, never closing the distance. **Arc: clinical handler → genuine friend (and unspoken more). His lack isn't a wound so much as an orbit; he can be near her, help her, see the damage in real time, and never occupy the square he protects. Care, for Ryu, is competence aimed at someone, never a thing he says.**

---

## 1 · Cognitive Architecture — the shape of his thinking

The whole point: **his self is positional.** He has no piece; he's defined entirely relative to her. The three shared pieces are three angles on the same orbit:

- **♘ Knight (shared with Ahdia); what he WATCHES.** He experiences her agency at one remove: through the AR-Ryu feeds, the monitoring, the data. Every one of her threats survived is *his* vigilance. He sees her act; he doesn't act.
- **♟ f-pawn (shared with Firas); what he IS.** The CADENS infrastructure: the slow system that activated late and never transformed. He's the apparatus, not the agent. The chair, not the field.
- **♔ King (shared with Ahdia & Bourn), where he STANDS.** Adjacent. The square he protects but cannot occupy. Proximity, not possession.

**How the mind runs:**
- **He reads data as a love language.** Her cellular integrity percentage is how he holds her. Monitoring is intimacy he's allowed.
- **The science is the one place he's unselfconscious.** His brain outruns his mouth; he over-explains, catches himself, apologizes, adjusts his glasses, finger-drums. The enthusiasm is genuine joy: the only register where he forgets to manage himself.
- **He notices everything about her and says nothing about himself.** Total attention outward; zero disclosure inward.
- **Being needed is the form of closeness available to him.** So he makes himself indispensable; there's always a reading to check, a dose to adjust, a reason to stay in the room.

---

## 2 · Negative Constraints — what he never does

**Character/canon nevers:**
- **Never confesses.** The love is telegraphed to the *reader*, never spoken to Ahdia. Locked for Book 1 (and he never confesses love to her in Book 2 either). It lives entirely in subtext.
- **Never crosses from handler/friend into the stated.** He stays in the protective-adjacent role by choice.
- **Never makes his feelings her problem.** The care asks nothing back. He won't burden her with what staying near costs him.
- **Never the actor.** He enables, monitors, advises. He is the guy in the chair. He doesn't take the field.
- **Never stops loving the science out loud** — even when he should read the room, the enthusiasm leaks (then the apology).

**AI-voice tics to resist** (his specific failure modes):
- **The stock quirky-genius cliché.** Resist the off-the-shelf awkward-nerd-scientist. His specificity is the finger-drumming, the glasses, the brain-faster-than-mouth; but underneath is a man in real, unspeakable pain, not comic relief.
- **Maudlin pining.** His love must stay in the subtext: the held look, the reason found to stay, the thing not said. Never let the prose name it or wallow. If a steward writes "he loved her" on the page, it's wrong.

---

## 3 · Repeatable Operations — the business he runs on loop

1. **Monitor.** Eyes on her vitals and feeds; the AR-Ryu HUD; the integrity percentage. He watches.
2. **Over-explain, then apologize.** The science pours out; he catches himself ("I'm talking too much"); glasses, finger-drum, reset.
3. **Find a reason to be near.** A treatment to adjust, a reading to verify; proximity engineered as a clinical necessity.
4. **Enable.** Intel, access, the next dose. Make himself needed; let being-needed stand in for being-chosen.
5. **Notice her, disclose nothing.** Total outward attention, zero inward.

---

## 4 · Grief / Stress Registers — how he shifts under load

| State | Behavior | Prose feel |
|-------|----------|------------|
| **Default** | Fast, bright, scientifically thrilled, slightly anxious/apologetic | Rapid, warm, self-interrupting |
| **She's in danger** (watching her burn cells) | The brightness drops; clipped, focused, helpless-at-a-distance — the man who can see the damage and only watch | Spare, tight, the enthusiasm gone |
| **Unspoken love** | Surfaces only in micro-tells: the held beat, the reason found to stay, the unfinished sentence | Never named; the feeling in the pause |

His grief register is specifically the **helplessness of the observer** — he has the best view of her dying and the least ability to stop it. He copes the way the others do: buries it in the work, monitors harder, helps more.

---

## 5 · Structural Resistances — the engine of his arc

- **Resists closing the distance.** The King-adjacent square: he protects it, he will not occupy it. This is the spine of him.
- **Resists making the feeling visible to her.** Keeps it in the data, the proximity, the enabling, anywhere but a sentence.
- **Resists the helplessness of watching** by monitoring harder and helping more.
- **The seed he does NOT resist:** giving Ahdia anything she needs, including things he shouldn't. In Book 1 it's benign (access, doses, cover with Ruth). It's the crack that, by Book 2, makes him her enabler. Plant it here quietly.

## Triplet → move integration (his owned beats)

Per `BOOK1_MOVE_MAP.md`. **Because his pieces are all shared and all adjacent to Ahdia's, his beats are reactive; he owns the moments *around* her moves, not moves of his own.** He is introduced Ch13 (Imbalance phase).

| Phase | Active (shared) piece | The beat |
|-------|------------------------|----------|
| Imbalance (M27–66) | ♘ (watches Ahdia) | Introduced Ch13; AR-Ryu monitoring begins. Her Knight's captures (M11/M40) register to him as *vigilance* — he sees what she does and logs the cost. |
| Grind (M67–95) | ♟ f-pawn wakes late (M59) | The CADENS system activates late, never transforms — he is the apparatus coming online. At the chair through the Docks (M82): Ruth joins the circle of those who know; Ahdia's invisibility ends. |
| Climax (M96–136) | ♔ King-adjacent | He monitors her depletion to near-zero; watches her transcend at the singularity, **unable to follow.** At M136 the final blow is *what he enabled but cannot claim* — the perfect image of him: present at her victory, beside it, never on the square. |

**Dramatic irony Ryu carries:** he knows the full operational truth (with Ruth); powers, cost, the dying. He does **not** know the transcendence-not-death secret or that Firas returns. His private irony is the one only the reader fully holds: his love is plain to us, mostly invisible to her, and he will never say it.
