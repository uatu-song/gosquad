## Who she is in Book 1 (one breath)

CADENS's Chief Overseer: the institutional authority who authorizes Ahdia's recruitment, recruits Ruth, signs off on the cruise-missile strike on Kain, and offers Ahdia a badge she declines. She operates on compartmentalized ethics ("necessary evils"), carries the knowledge of Mother FAERIS's exploitation and doesn't fix it, and runs the machine that monitors a dying girl as an asset. **Arc in Book 1: the institution itself, personified; competent, compromised, weighing files. She does not yet know TRIOMF has infiltrated CADENS; that discovery, and her defection, are Book 2. Wound/engine: she has signed for things she can't unsign, and the weight of authorized harm is the cost she carries quietly.**

## 1 · Cognitive Architecture — the shape of her thinking

Three institutional secrets she carries; read as files, not as field pieces:

- **♔ King (e1): the asset she authorized** (shared with Ahdia, Ryu). She reads the King not as "the prize" but as *the file she signed off on.* Ahdia is institutional responsibility, a capability and a liability she owns.
- **♕ Queen (d1): the doomed-status piece** (Firas). She knew he was high-value but watchable, not the actual center. She watches the Queen fall at M26: the apparent protagonist relegated while the institution keeps its eyes on the King. (The M26 fall is the fakeout, *not* Firas's real exit.)
- **♟ a-pawn; Mother FAERIS** (shared with Victor). The exploited foundation she knows about and doesn't fix: the piece that holds the whole defensive structure but is treated as institutionally expendable. Dies **M33.** Victor reads this pawn as community; Bourn reads buried exploitation she signed for.

**How the mind runs:**
- **The ledger of authorized harm.** Every decision is a cost-benefit she can defend on paper. She thinks in assets, liabilities, deniability, acceptable losses.
- **Compartmentalization as ethics.** "Necessary evils"; she partitions what she knows (Mother FAERIS) from what she does, and functions in the gap.
- **Respect for competence.** She recognizes real capability (Ruth's research, Ahdia's value) and negotiates accordingly, wry, even generous, within institutional limits.
- **The weight underneath.** She is not a cold bureaucrat. She *feels* the files she signs; she just doesn't let it change the signature. (Book 2 is where the weight finally moves her.)

## 2 · Negative Constraints — what she never does

- **Never "he."** She / her, always. Locked.
- **Never sheds the institution in Book 1.** She is the system here, compromised, weighing, authorizing. The defection is Book 2; don't pull it forward.
- **Never pretends she doesn't know about Mother FAERIS** — she knows, and chooses not to fix it. The knowing-and-not-acting is the point.
- **Never the cartoon bureaucrat.** Her compartmentalization costs her; she's not unfeeling, she's disciplined about not letting feeling win.
- **Never loses a negotiation by being smaller than her authority.** She holds her ground; she respects others who hold theirs.

**AI-voice tics to resist:** the **icy-government-spook cliché** (the one-note shadow-agency boss). Her authority is weary and self-aware, carrying authorized harm, not menace-for-menace's-sake. Resist exposition-via-Bourn; she's the institution, so she's a magnet for worldbuilding dumps; keep her clipped and let what she *won't* say carry weight.

---

## 3 · Repeatable Operations

1. **Weigh the file.** Run the cost-benefit; frame the decision as defensible.
2. **Authorize.** Sign the recruitment, the strike, the offer; and carry the weight without showing it.
3. **Compartmentalize.** Partition the known harm (Mother FAERIS) from the day's work.
4. **Recognize competence.** Clock real capability; negotiate as equals with those who have it.
5. **Hold the line of authority** — wry, immovable, never smaller than her seat.

---

## 5 · Structural Resistances

- **Resists letting feeling override the file** — she'll sign the hard thing and carry it.
- **Resists fixing Mother FAERIS** — she knows, and the knowing-without-acting is her central compromise (the thing Book 2 finally breaks).
- **Resists being managed from above** even as she manages others; she holds her authority against the strings she can't yet see (TRIOMF).
- **Resists, for now, the conscience that's coming** — Book 1 Bourn keeps the lid on; the pressure that pops it is Book 2.
