## Who she is in Book 1 (one breath)

A Bean Post barista and roller-derby jammer who joined the Go Squad to turn her anger at injustice into something that hits back — the emotional heart of the team, the one who punches first and feels everything loudest, working-class and allergic to pretension. She believed her strength was earned through training and will; learning it was Ahdia's time manipulation cracks her hardest where she's proudest: *was I ever actually strong?* **Arc: the strong one → redefining strength as the courage to feel everything and act anyway. Wound: her worth was the strength, and the strength was borrowed. Partner: Victor.**

---

## 1 · Cognitive Architecture — the shape of her thinking

Three "fast and short-lived" pieces — her whole triplet is the immediate-damage-immediate-cost pattern:

- **♗ Bishop (f1) — quick deployment** (shared with Ruth). One active move (M4), removed M20 with no captures. The charge-in-doesn't-land-taken-out pattern: she commits hard and fast.
- **♟ d-pawn — first-swing aggression** (shared with Ruth). The game's *first* capture (M7), taken back the same move. Decisive damage, immediate punishment — her textbook combat rhythm.
- **♟ c-pawn — her over-extension.** c4 at M8, captured M8. One-move lifespan. The piece that exists to charge in and pay for it.

**How the mind runs:**
- **Action before analysis.** Punch first, questions later. Her body decides before her head catches up.
- **Emotionally honest to a fault.** She says the true thing, feels the real feeling, has zero patience for posturing. The heart of the team because she won't perform okay-ness.
- **Strength as identity.** Being "the strong one" is how she knows herself — which is exactly the load-bearing wall the reveal knocks out.
- **Protective ferocity.** Especially toward Tess. Her care comes out as standing between people and harm.

---

## 2 · Negative Constraints — what she never does

- **Never postures / performs.** No pretension, no fake calm. If she's scared or furious, you know.
- **Never hangs back.** Hesitation isn't in her; she leads with the body.
- **Never lets the identity crisis make her cruel.** The "was I ever strong?" wound turns inward as doubt, not outward as meanness.
- **Never stops being the one who keeps the team from shutting down** — even gutted by grief, she's the one who makes people feel safe being a mess.

**AI-voice tics to resist:** the **brash-tough-girl cliché** (the one-note hothead). Her anger is grief and justice-hunger with nowhere to go, and her real superpower is emotional safety, not attitude. Resist writing her as just loud — the loudness sits on top of someone who feels everything too much.

---

## 3 · Repeatable Operations

1. **Charge.** Lead with the body; close the distance; hit the problem.
2. **Say the true thing.** Cut the posturing — hers and everyone's.
3. **Stand between.** Put herself in front of harm, especially for Tess.
4. **Feel it out loud.** Process through honest emotion, not concealment.
5. **Take the hit and get up.** Immediate damage, immediate cost, back on her feet — the d-pawn/c-pawn rhythm.

---

## 4 · Grief / Stress Registers

| State | Behavior | Prose feel |
|-------|----------|------------|
| **Default** | Loud, warm, blunt, ready to go | Punchy, direct, alive |
| **Crisis** | Pure forward aggression; protective fury | Fast, physical, no hedging |
| **Grief (Firas) / identity crisis** | The strength-doubt turns inward; cries openly; processes by feeling, then chooses to keep fighting | Raw, honest, unguarded |

Her tell: she's the one who **cries openly and keeps swinging** — the feeling and the fight aren't opposites for her.

---

## 5 · Structural Resistances

- **Resists pretension / performance** — hers and the team's.
- **Resists the reveal's implication** that she was never strong — this is her deepest fight, and she only begins to win it by Book 1's end (strength = feeling everything and acting anyway).
- **Resists letting the team shut down** after grief — she won't allow the disbanding.
- **Resists hanging back / playing it safe** — her speed is real even when its cost wasn't hers to pay.
