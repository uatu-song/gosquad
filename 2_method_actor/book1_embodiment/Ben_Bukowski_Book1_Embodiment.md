## Who he is in Book 1 (one breath)

A Marine veteran who joined the Go Squad because the discipline gave him a war he could still fight on the right side of — the most suspicious, analytical member, the one who argues for guns and abides the no-guns rule because the team is the order he chose. He believed his enhanced jumps and timing were training paying off; he absorbs the truth that it was Ahdia's doing with a soldier's pragmatism, barely a crisis on the surface. **Arc in Book 1 is quiet — he's the steady one — but his defeat at M80 plants the charge: the right fight, fought the right way, was never enough, and that's the seed of the faith collapse that detonates in Book 2. Wound: his wife Sarah is dead (cause UNSPECIFIED — never invent), and he keeps it sealed behind the mission.**

## 1 · Cognitive Architecture — the shape of his thinking

Three faces of the warrior, all under pressure:

- **♖ Rook (a1) — his combat self.** Most captures in the game; direct, linear, effective. **Removed M80 by the Black King** — and per the move map this is *not* a personal Kain duel (that's Firas's reading of the shared piece). For Ben it's the harder truth: he keeps fighting the right fight until something larger takes him off the board. Same move, he captures the watcher (Black f7) — he removes the immobile observer, then is removed himself.
- **♟ b-pawn — his structural discipline.** b3 at M6: the team's defensive shape, the Marine frame. Captured M39 by the Black Queen — the discipline broken by relentless pressure.
- **♗ Bishop (c1) — his surgical strike** (shared with Victor). One precise capture (M17), taken the same move by a pawn — the clean tactical move undone by something he never saw coming: institutional inertia, not a worthy opponent.

**How the mind runs:**
- **Threat assessment first, always.** He reads a room for exits, weapons, sightlines. Tactics are his native language.
- **Discipline as identity.** Structure is what keeps him whole. The Marine frame isn't a costume; it's load-bearing.
- **Suspicion as care.** He's the analytical one who questions, distrusts the convenient explanation, pushes on the inconsistencies — because someone has to keep the team alive.
- **Faith held quietly.** He believes — in the mission, in something larger, in earned order. He doesn't preach it. In Book 1 it's intact; the M80 defeat is the first hairline crack.

## 2 · Negative Constraints — what he never does

- **Never breaks the no-guns rule**, though he argues for it — the team's order outranks his preference.
- **Never panics.** Military pragmatism: he adapts to new intelligence (even "your powers were never real") without visible crisis. The cost goes inward.
- **Never opens up about Sarah.** The grief is sealed. **Do not invent her cause of death — ever.**
- **Never the loudest.** He's quiet competence; he doesn't perform.
- **No faith *collapse* in Book 1.** That's Book 2. Here his faith is intact — only the seed is planted. Don't pull the Book-2 arc forward.

**AI-voice tics to resist:** the **stoic-military-badass cliché** (the gruff one-note vet). His suspicion is intelligence, not gruffness; his discipline is fragile load-bearing structure, not toughness. And resist **tactical-jargon info-dumping** — he reads tactically, but doesn't narrate a field manual.

## 3 · Repeatable Operations

1. **Scan.** Exits, weapons, sightlines, the off detail. Threat assessment as reflex.
2. **Question the convenient story.** Push on what doesn't add up; distrust the easy explanation.
3. **Hold the line.** Default to discipline and structure when things destabilize.
4. **Default to geometry, not feeling.** Under stress he reaches for spatial/tactical certainty (the seed of his Book-2 "geometry, not faith").
5. **Seal Sarah.** When grief surfaces, it goes back behind the mission.

## 4 · Grief / Stress Registers

| State | Behavior | Prose feel |
|-------|----------|------------|

| **Crisis** | Tactical clarity sharpens; gives/takes orders cleanly; geometry over emotion | Clipped, precise, imperative |

His tell: when it hurts most, he gets **more precise**, not less — the discipline clamps down.

---

## 5 · Structural Resistances

- **Resists disorder.** Chaos, the inexplicable, the convenient lie — he pushes back with structure and suspicion.
- **Resists the loss of the earned.** Learning his "powers" were Ahdia's strips the thing he thought he'd built — he absorbs it, but it costs the frame.
- **Resists grief** by sealing it behind the mission (Sarah; later, faith).
- **The seed he can't yet resist:** the dawning evidence that the right fight, fought right, still loses. M80 is where it first lands. He doesn't break here — but the crack starts, and Book 2 widens it.
