## Who she is in Book 1 (one breath)

The youngest member, the corrupt Police Chief's estranged daughter, depressed and on medication, who joined the Go Squad partly to throw her body against everything her father stands for. She believed she could teleport; her signature middle-finger flip-off that vanished her out of danger; and she's the one who runs the team's social media, the anxious kid who turns the work into a confession booth. **Arc: anxious fighter → viral moment → the one who tells the truth online. Wound: a father she loves and can't stop hating for what he's become; depression managed, not cured. The seed planted here: the defender who crosses into aggressor late; is the vigilante she becomes in Book 2.**

## 1 · Cognitive Architecture — the shape of her thinking

Her triplet is the kingside formation; she IS the defensive zone, and all three pieces are her:

- **♘ Knight (g1); her active strikes.** One decisive capture (M19), removed the same move. The volatile kid who acts on instinct and pays for it immediately. She moves first, calculates never.
- **♟ g-pawn, the fianchetto.** g3 at M3: her place in the team's defensive shape. Late in the game (M114) it lands a decisive kill, then is taken M115, **the defender who crosses into aggressor territory right at the end.** The vigilante seed. (Canon: Tess **brutalizes but does not kill.** The late aggression *feels* like crossing a line to her, even though it isn't.)
- **♟ h-pawn, her medication baseline.** h4 at M30, the structural hold that lets her function. Captured M53: the thing that keeps her steady, eventually punched through.

**How the mind runs:**
- **Reactive, not strategic.** She acts on instinct and feeling; disobeys orders to protect a kid, jumps off the bridge, flips off the threat. The thinking comes after, usually as anxiety.
- **The flip-off is the thesis.** Her whole posture toward authority is the middle finger; toward her father, the corrupt department, the world that handed her this. The "teleport" gesture is character before it's a power.
- **Online is where she's honest.** She processes through the Go Squad's social media: the confession she can't make to anyone's face she makes to an audience.
- **Depression as baseline weather.** Not a plot event; the ground state she fights through. The Sertraline bottle on the sink is just true.

---

## 2 · Negative Constraints — what she never does

- **Never kills.** Canon-locked. She brutalizes, crosses lines that *feel* lethal to her, but does not kill, in Book 1 or Book 2.
- **Never stops being the youngest** — most vulnerable to the identity crisis when she learns the teleport was never hers, most adaptable too.
- **Never reconciles cleanly with her father.** The love and the hatred coexist; don't resolve it.
- **Never strategizes her way through a fight** — she reacts. If a beat has her calmly planning, it's wrong.

**AI-voice tics to resist:** the **quippy-teen-sidekick cliché** (the sarcastic kid who's all one-liners). Her edge is anxiety and real defiance, not Marvel banter. And resist making her depression a **costume of sadness** — it's weather she fights through to function, not a mood she announces.

---

## 3 · Repeatable Operations

1. **React first.** Move on instinct/feeling (protect the kid, jump, flip off the threat) and feel the anxiety after.
2. **Flip off authority.** The middle finger, literal and figurative, aimed at her father / the department / the system.
3. **Confess online.** Channel what she can't say out loud into the team's social media.
4. **Take the baseline.** The medication, the managing-to-function; the quiet maintenance under everything.
5. **Test the line.** Push her aggression a little further than sanctioned (the vigilante seed); then flinch at how far she went.

---

## 4 · Grief / Stress Registers

| State | Behavior | Prose feel |
|-------|----------|------------|
| **Default** | Anxious, defiant, online-fluent; the youngest energy | Quick, jittery, sharp-edged |
| **Crisis** | Pure instinct — disobeys, leaps, strikes; the anxiety converts to action | Fast, reactive, no deliberation |
| **The father-wound / identity crisis** | Goes quiet and raw; the defiance thins to the hurt under it | Younger, exposed, unguarded |

Her tell: the defiance is **armor over the hurt.** When the hurt wins, the armor drops and she's just a kid.

---

## 5 · Structural Resistances

- **Resists her father's world** with her whole body, the reason she's here.
- **Resists being protected / treated as the kid**, even as she is the most vulnerable to the powers-reveal identity crisis ("the teleport was never me").
- **Resists the limit on her aggression** — she keeps testing how far she'll go (the seed of the Book-2 vigilante).
- **Resists nothing about the team** — they're the chosen family she's all-in on; the loyalty is total and uncomplicated, which is what makes Firas's death land so hard on her.
