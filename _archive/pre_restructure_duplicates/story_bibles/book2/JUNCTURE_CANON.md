# Book 2 — Juncture Canon

## Purpose

This document records the agreed-upon narrative details for every interaction point between characters in the Book 2 chess scaffold. When two or more characters share an event (capture, threat, witness), this canon defines **what happened, how it happened, and what it means**. Individual steward sessions have total creative latitude for their character's internal experience, but the external facts of each juncture are locked.

## Key Rule

**CAPTURE = REMOVAL FROM FIELD, NOT DEATH.** The form of each removal is specified below.

---

## Juncture 1 — M4: Bellatrix's Pawn Captures Leah+Victor (bxc4)

**External facts:** The position at c4 was a prepared trap. Kain's forces anticipated an aggressive push into that square. Leah and Victor walked into it. Leah is incapacitated — not killed. Victor is not incapacitated — he survives and migrates to Ben's unit.

**Constraints:**

- This was systemic, not personal. No individual villain targeted Leah specifically. The trap would have caught whoever pushed forward first.
- Victor saw it coming a half-second before it sprung. He could not stop it.
- Leah had some awareness of what was happening as it happened.

---

## Juncture 2 — M10: Ruth+Tess (Nxc6) Capture Bellatrix's Pawn

**External facts:** Tess identifies the c6 weakness before Ruth does. Tess makes the call to strike. Ruth follows. They remove Bellatrix's defensive pawn, breaking Black's queenside coordination. Prime Kain witnesses this from d7.

**Constraints:**

- Ruth is not the one who initiates this action. Tess is.
- Ruth's judgment is affected by Leah's fall at M4.
- This is the moment that redefines Ruth and Tess as an equal partnership rather than leader and sidekick.

---

## Juncture 3 — M19: Kain Clone (Bxf3) Captures Ben+Victor's Pawn Companion

**External facts:** Kain clone removes Ben+Victor's defensive resource. This is his second kill. He does not engage Ben or Victor directly — he strips their infrastructure. The king position opens as a result.

**Constraints:**

- This follows the same pattern as M17 (his first kill). Systematic elimination, not combat.
- The loss is structural, not violent. Ben and Victor experience erosion, not attack.
- Victor has seen this pattern before — it echoes M4.

---

## Juncture 4 — M21: Ruth+Tess (Nxh1) Capture Kain Clone

**External facts:** Ruth and Tess remove the Kain clone. The clone's consciousness transfers — the body was always expendable. Ruth and Tess end up on h1, completely out of position. Three moves later, Bellatrix removes them from the field. Their pursuit of the clone is what puts them in that vulnerable position.

**Constraints:**

- The clone is not surprised or distressed by removal. This was built into the plan.
- Ruth and Tess achieve their objective AND compromise their position in the same action.
- The clone did what he was built to do. Three kills, then one final cost — pulling Ruth and Tess out of position.

---

## Juncture 5 — M24: Bellatrix (Qxg3) Captures Ruth+Tess

**External facts:** Bellatrix personally removes Ruth and Tess from the field. The removal is not violent. Ruth and Tess do not go down fighting. They have been active since M3 — 21 moves of engagement ends here, two moves before checkmate.

**Constraints:**

- The removal is not brutal, not contemptuous, not casual. It has weight.
- Bellatrix is a 3.7-billion-year-old consciousness. Whatever she does in this moment should reflect that scale.
- Ruth and Tess are together when they're removed. They entered as a pair, they leave as a pair.
- This removal is what forces Ahdia to deliver checkmate alone.

---

## Juncture 6 — M26: Ahdia (Qxd7#) Captures Prime Kain — CHECKMATE

**External facts:** Ahdia moves to d7 and captures Prime Kain, delivering checkmate. The checkmate is not a spectacle. It is quiet. It is the final move of the game.

**Constraints:**

- Prime Kain has been static since M8. He let others fight for him. He is ended by someone he never accounted for.
- Ahdia is experiencing significant cellular degradation at this point. She is near the transcendence boundary.
- This is Ahdia's only capture in the entire game. One kill. The one that ends everything.
- Ahdia has been repositioning the entire game (Qa4, Qb5, Qc4, Qc3, Qc6) — constant movement, never settled, until this single decisive strike.

---

## Eidolon's Solo Moment — M25: Rb8??

**External facts:** Eidolon makes one move the entire game. Black is winning at -8.89. Eidolon moves Rb8. The evaluation flips to forced checkmate for White. This single move exposes Prime Kain to Ahdia's queen and enables Qc6 → Qxd7#.

**Constraints:**

- Eidolon is a fear entity. Its nature should inform why it makes this move.
- The blunder is not stupidity. It comes from somewhere essential to what Eidolon is.
- Eidolon survives the game. It is never captured. But its action destroyed its own side.
- Without this move, Ahdia has no checkmate. Eidolon is directly responsible for the outcome.
