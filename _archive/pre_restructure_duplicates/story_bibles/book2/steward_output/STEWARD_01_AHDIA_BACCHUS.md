# STEWARD OUTLINE: AHDIA BACCHUS
## Book 2 — White Queen (d1)

---

### PRE-ENTRY: M1–M10 (Ahdia is off the board)

**Beat 1: Watching from d1**
Ahdia is not on the field. She's in the command position — rooftop, penthouse, CADENS comms — receiving intelligence. She is the queen who hasn't moved yet. The game is happening around her. She can see all of it and touch none of it.

What she knows: Leah and Victor deployed early (Bf1→c4, M2). Ruth and Tess mobilized (Nc3, M3). Bourn and Ryu are in institutional holding pattern (Ra1, static). Ben is in reserve (Bc1, static). The operation is underway. Her people are in the field. She is not.

**Internal:** This is familiar. This is the apartment. This is twelve hours of television and the knowledge that the world is turning without her. Except now she has comms. Now she has FAERIS feeds. Now she can see in real-time the thing she used to only imagine — other people doing the living she couldn't. The difference between depression-isolation and strategic-reserve feels thinner than she'd like to admit.

She'd frame it like a procedural. *The handler stays in the van. The handler always stays in the van. In every show she's ever watched, the handler stays in the van until the moment the handler has to not stay in the van, and then someone dies.*

**Beat 2: M4 — Leah goes down (bxc4)**
Ahdia receives the report: Leah incapacitated. Victor survived, migrating to Ben's position. The trap at c4 was systemic — not targeted, just waiting for whoever pushed first. Leah pushed first.

Ahdia is not on the board. She did not witness this. She hears it through comms, through FAERIS data, through Ryu's voice going careful and clinical the way it does when the numbers are bad.

**Internal:** She could have been there. If she'd deployed earlier — if the queen moved on move 2 instead of move 11 — she could have seen it coming. Time manipulation means she could have frozen the trap mid-spring and pulled Leah out. But the queen doesn't move that early. The queen doesn't move that early because if the queen moves that early, the queen gets captured, and then who delivers checkmate?

She knows this is the math. She hates that she's good at the math.

*It's the trolley problem, except the trolley is her, and the people on the tracks are her friends, and the lever is just... staying in the van.*

**Beat 3: M10 — Ruth and Tess strike (Nxc6)**
Ruth and Tess break Bellatrix's queenside coordination. Tess made the call — not Ruth. This registers. Ahdia files it. Tess is operating differently now.

Prime Kain witnesses from d7. He's been sitting there since M8. Ahdia notes this too. The one who doesn't move. The one who lets everyone else bleed for him.

**Internal:** She recognizes him. Not personally — categorically. She's been that person. The one who sits while others act. Except she sat because she was broken, and he sits because he's protected. Same posture. Different reason. She doesn't like that the silhouette matches.

This is the moment she starts planning. Not reacting — *planning*. Ruth and Tess are deep in enemy territory. Ben and Victor are holding. The Kain clone is stripping infrastructure. She can see the shape of what's coming. She's watched enough television to know what the third act looks like. The hero team pushes too far. The support gets stripped. Someone has to do the thing no one else can do. Alone.

*Move 11. She's going in.*

---

### M11: Qa4 (d1 → a4) — ENTRY

**External:** Ahdia deploys. First move. Queen to a4 — a lateral repositioning, not an attack. She doesn't charge into the center. She slides to the flank. Wide angle. Survey position.

**Internal:** The apartment door opens. This is the moment in every show where the camera pushes in on the face and the score drops to a single sustained note. She's been watching for ten moves. She knows the board. She knows who's hurt and who's holding and who's bleeding out slowly in ways they haven't noticed yet.

She deploys to a4 because a4 sees everything. The whole queenside. She can see Ruth and Tess operating deep. She can see the structural erosion the Kain clone is performing. She can see Prime Kain on d7, not moving, not needing to move.

**Temporal cost:** First bubble activation. Minor nosebleed. She wipes it on her sleeve before anyone on comms can ask. Ryu's HUD data pings a 2% baseline fluctuation. He notes it. He doesn't say anything yet.

**Connection to next beat:** She needs to see more. a4 is a vantage point, not a position. She's going to keep moving.

---

### M12 (Ahdia does not move — gap)

**External:** Between her moves, the game continues. M12: Nd5 / Qd6. Ruth and Tess reposition (Nd5). Bellatrix's queen repositions (Qd6). The board is tightening.

**Internal:** She holds position at a4. Watches. The nosebleed stopped but the taste of copper is still in her mouth. She has a tissue from the emergency kit Ryu insisted she carry. It's the kind of detail Ruth would notice and Ryu would document and she would ignore.

*In procedurals, the competent handler radios calm instructions while the field team does the hard part. She's radioing nothing. She's doing math. She's counting squares between herself and Prime Kain and finding the number too large and the path too blocked and the cost too high — for now.*

---

### M13: Qb5 (a4 → b5) — REPOSITIONING

**External:** Queen to b5. Diagonal slide, still on the flank but closer to the center. She's tightening the angle. Not attacking — *narrowing*.

**Internal:** Every move she makes is a time bubble. Every time bubble is cellular degradation. a4 to b5 is one square diagonal but it's another activation, another nosebleed she doesn't report, another data point on Ryu's HUD that he logs and doesn't share.

b5 puts her in line with d7. She can see Prime Kain now. Not through intermediaries, not through FAERIS feeds — she can see the square he's sitting on. He hasn't moved since M8. He won't move again. She knows this the way she knows which character in a crime drama is the real killer: the one the camera never follows. The one who's always in the room but never in the frame.

**Connection:** She's building a diagonal. a4 → b5 → c4 → ... → the line terminates at d7 eventually, but not directly. Not yet. She needs the board to open. She needs other pieces to clear the path. She needs to keep moving and keep not-arriving.

---

### M14 (gap) — M18 (gap): THE LONG WATCH

**External:** Five moves of game time where Ahdia holds at b5, then repositions to c4 at M15 and holds again. During this window:
- M14: gxf3 — White recaptures, opening the kingside. Structural damage.
- M17: Bxc6 — Kain clone's first kill. Strips an unassigned white knight. Systematic.
- M18: dxe5/fxe5 — Pawn exchanges. Lines opening. The board is becoming skeletal.

**Beat — M15: Qc4 (b5 → c4)**
Queen to c4. She's in the center now. Not the flank, not the vantage — the center. Leah was incapacitated on c4. The same square. Ahdia occupies the space where her friend fell.

**Internal:** She didn't plan this. The math brought her here. c4 is the optimal square for coverage and future mobility and she occupies it because it's correct, and it's also the square where Leah went down six moves ago, and she stands on it and feels nothing and then feels everything and then files the everything into the same locked cabinet where she keeps the warehouse and the police ambush and every other thing she processes later, in frozen time, alone, where no one can see her face.

*There's a trope. The detective who returns to the crime scene. Who stands where the body was found. Who looks at the chalk outline and sees something the first responders missed. Ahdia is standing on Leah's chalk outline and what she sees is: the board is almost ready.*

**Temporal cost:** Third activation (M15). Bleeding from the left ear now. Ryu's HUD shows a 6% baseline drop across three activations. Ruth, in the field, notices something in Ahdia's voice on comms. A roughness. A catch. Ruth asks if she's okay. Ahdia says she's fine. This is the first lie of the endgame.

**Beat — M17–M19: Watching the Kain Clone work**
From c4, Ahdia watches the Kain clone systematically strip White's position. M17: takes the unassigned knight. M19: takes Ben and Victor's f-pawn companion. No direct combat. Infrastructure elimination. She recognizes the pattern because it's what she would do — what she *is* doing, from the other side. Patient. Structural. Impersonal.

The Kain clone is her mirror. Both of them are doing the same thing: methodical repositioning while the flashier pieces fight. The difference is that the clone is expendable and knows it. Ahdia is expendable and pretends she isn't.

---

### M19: Qc3 (c4 → c3) — RETREAT / RECOIL

**External:** Queen to c3. She pulls back one rank. c4 → c3. Backward. This is the same move where Juncture 3 occurs: Kain clone captures Ben+Victor's pawn companion (Bxf3). The king position opens.

**Internal:** She retreats to c3 and the retreat is real. Not strategic ambiguity — actual retreat. The board is falling apart. Ruth and Tess are overextended. Ben and Victor are losing infrastructure. The Kain clone just opened the king position. The math she's been doing since M11 is resolving and the answer is worse than she projected.

c3 is a defensive square. c3 is the queen saying: *I need one more move before I can do what I came to do.* She's been repositioning for eight moves (M11–M19) and she still isn't ready. The path to d7 isn't clear yet. She needs the board to break open further. She needs — she hates this — she needs more of her own side to be removed.

**Temporal cost:** Fourth activation. Vision blurring in the right eye. She's been operating past Ryu's recommended thresholds since M15. The math says she has two more activations before she hits the danger zone. Two more moves. She needs exactly two more moves.

*She thinks about the shows where the protagonist calculates their remaining ammunition. Counts the bullets. Always one short. Always exactly one short until the moment they're not, because fiction is kinder than math. She has two bullets. She needs two bullets. For once, the math is kind.*

But the math is only kind if she doesn't factor in what the bullets cost. And she can't afford to factor that in. Not yet.

**Connection:** Six moves of gap between M19 and M25. The longest wait. Ruth and Tess are about to pursue the Kain clone (M21), get overextended to h1, and Bellatrix will remove them at M24. Ahdia is going to watch all of this from c3.

---

### M20–M24: THE COLLAPSE (Ahdia holds at c3)

**Beat — M21: Ruth and Tess capture the Kain clone (Nxh1)**
*Juncture 4.* Ruth and Tess remove the clone. The clone's consciousness transfers — the body was always expendable. Ruth and Tess end up on h1. Completely out of position. Their success is their vulnerability.

Ahdia doesn't witness this from adjacency — she's on c3, across the board. She sees it through FAERIS data. Through the comms going sharp with adrenaline and then going quiet with the wrong kind of quiet. Ruth and Tess achieved their objective AND compromised their position in the same action. Both/and.

**Internal:** She knows what's coming. She's seen this episode. The one where the field team takes out the decoy and celebrates for half a second and then the camera pulls back to show the real threat approaching from behind. She wants to scream into comms: *Get out of h1. Get out of h1 right now.* But she's the queen on c3 and she can't reach them and the math says if she moves now — if she spends an activation pulling them out — she won't have enough left for the checkmate.

*The trolley problem again. Except the trolley is Bellatrix, and the people on the tracks are Ruth and Tess, and the lever is the checkmate she can't deliver if she pulls it.*

She doesn't pull the lever. She stays on c3.

**Beat — M22: Ben and Victor make their single move (Be3)**
Ben's unit finally acts. One move. The right move. Be3 shores up the defense and enables the endgame. Ahdia notes this from c3. The reserve activated at exactly the right moment. Victor's analytical framework found the timing. Ben's discipline held until it was needed.

**Internal:** One move. They waited twenty-two moves and made one move and it was the right one. She thinks about Ben's Marine discipline. About Victor watching from M4 onward, carrying the grief of Leah's fall, waiting, calculating, and then: *now.* She respects it in a way that feels like looking in a mirror. Patient operators. People who understand that the right single action outweighs twenty wrong ones.

**Beat — M22: Qh4+ — Bellatrix gives check**
The climax. Bellatrix's queen drives into White's position. Check. The king is under direct threat. Everything before this was positioning. Now it's existential.

**Internal:** She feels the check from c3. Not physically — the check is on the king, not her — but the way a tremor runs through a building before the earthquake. The fundamental structure of the board just shifted. Bellatrix is no longer accumulating material. Bellatrix is hunting.

**Temporal cost awareness:** She's at M22. She estimated two more activations at M19. If she activates now, she has one left. One for the checkmate. She cannot activate now. She has to hold through the climax without acting. She has to watch.

**Beat — M23–M24: Bellatrix takes everything (Qxh2, then Qxg3)**
Bellatrix's queen rampages. Takes h2. Then takes g3 — *Juncture 5.* Ruth and Tess are removed from the field.

Per the juncture canon: The removal is not violent. Not brutal, not contemptuous, not casual. It has weight. Bellatrix is 3.7 billion years old. Whatever she does reflects that scale. Ruth and Tess are together when they're removed. They entered as a pair, they leave as a pair.

Ahdia does not witness this from adjacency. She is on c3. She receives the data. FAERIS feeds. Comms going to static. Ryu's voice — tight, professional, breaking — confirming that Ruth and Tess are off the board.

**Internal:** This is the moment the van door opens. This is the moment the handler stops being the handler. Not because the plan changed — because the plan *worked*. This was always the shape. She's been doing the math since M11 and the math always ended here: alone on c3 with two activations left and no one between her and Prime Kain except the squares she has to cross.

Ruth is gone. The person who advocated for her. The person who said *she should be on this team*. The person who noticed the ear bleeding and asked if she was okay and accepted the lie because what else can you do when you're in the field and the answer to "are you okay" is "I'm dying slowly and every time I use my powers it accelerates and I calculated the exact number of activations I could afford and I'm on the last two."

Tess is gone. The one who shares the Sertraline. The one who understands that sometimes getting out of bed is the whole fight.

She's alone on c3. The board is almost empty. Bellatrix holds +19 material advantage. Prime Kain is still on d7. He hasn't moved in sixteen moves.

*She thinks: this is the part of the show where the music drops out. Where the protagonist stands in the wreckage and the sound design goes to a single high tone and the camera pushes in on the face and there's nothing left except the one thing that has to happen next.*

She doesn't feel heroic. She feels like the last person in the room at a party that ended badly. She feels like the one who stayed because someone had to lock up.

---

### M25: Qc6 (c3 → c6) — THE PENULTIMATE MOVE

**External:** Ahdia moves to c6. Deep into Black's territory. This move is enabled entirely by Eidolon's blunder: Rb8?? (Eidolon's solo moment, per juncture canon). Without Rb8, Ahdia has no path to checkmate. The fear entity overreached, and in doing so, exposed Prime Kain to the queen's diagonal.

Qc6 is the setup. It puts Ahdia one move from d7. One move from Prime Kain. One move from the end.

**Internal:** The fear entity moved. One move. Its only move in the entire game. And it was the wrong one.

She doesn't know why Eidolon made the move. She doesn't know what a fear entity thinks or feels or calculates. But she knows this: fear overreaches. That's what fear *does*. It amplifies, it distorts, it drives action that looks aggressive and is actually desperate. Eidolon projected force — Rb8 — and in projecting force, it uncovered the thing it was supposed to protect.

*She's watched this in a hundred horror movies. The thing in the dark is scariest when you can't see it. The moment it steps into the light, it's just a thing. Eidolon stepped into the light. And in the light, there's a line from c6 to d7 that wasn't there before.*

**Temporal cost:** Fifth activation. She is past every threshold Ryu ever set. The nosebleed is constant now. Both ears. Vision in the right eye is gone — not blurred, *gone*. She is operating on spatial memory and FAERIS data and the kind of focus that only happens when the body has decided that this is the last thing it's going to do, so it might as well do it perfectly.

The cellular baseline is approaching transcendence territory. She can feel it — not as pain, but as *thinning*. Like the boundary between her and not-her is becoming negotiable. Like the Hyper Seed is asking a question and the question is: *do you want to stay in this form?*

She doesn't answer. She has one more move.

**Connection:** One move. c6 → d7. One activation. One capture. One checkmate. Everything since M11 — every repositioning, every nosebleed, every lie to Ruth, every moment of holding still while her people fell — converges on one square.

---

### M26: Qxd7# — CHECKMATE

**External:** *Juncture 6.* Ahdia moves to d7 and captures Prime Kain, delivering checkmate. The checkmate is not a spectacle. It is quiet. It is the final move of the game.

Per canon: Prime Kain has been static since M8. He let others fight for him. He is ended by someone he never accounted for. Ahdia is experiencing significant cellular degradation. She is near the transcendence boundary. This is her only capture in the entire game. One kill. The one that ends everything.

She has been repositioning the entire game — Qa4, Qb5, Qc4, Qc3, Qc6 — constant movement, never settled, until this single decisive strike.

**Internal:** d7.

She's here.

He's here. He's been here since move 8. Eighteen moves of not moving. Eighteen moves of other people fighting, other people dying, other people being removed from the field — and he sat on d7 and let it happen because he had a queen and a clone and a fear entity and +19 material advantage and why would he ever need to move?

She captures him. One action. Force amplification in a time bubble so precise it makes every previous activation look like finger painting. She doesn't hit hard. She hits *exactly*. The removal is surgical. It is quiet. It is the final move of the game.

The checkmate is not for him. It's not even against him. Prime Kain is a square she had to reach. A coordinate. The checkmate is the board resolving — twenty-six moves of positioning and sacrifice and loss collapsing into a single point where the geometry says: *it's over.*

**Temporal cost — the bill:**
Sixth activation. The big one. The capture. Force amplification.

The cellular baseline drops. She doesn't know the number. Ryu would know the number. Ruth would know the number. Neither of them is here. She's alone on d7 and the Hyper Seed is no longer asking. It's *offering*. The boundary between human and something-else is a membrane now, not a wall, and she can feel herself pressing against it the way you press against a window — not to break it, just because the glass is warm and the other side looks quiet.

She doesn't cross. She *chooses* not to cross. Not because she's not ready — because the game is over and there are people on this side of the glass who need to know it's over. Ruth, removed but alive. Tess, removed but alive. Ben and Victor, surviving. Leah, incapacitated but alive. Ryu, somewhere, with a HUD full of data that says she should be dead.

She stays. Not because she's worthy of staying. Not because she's earned it. She stays because staying is the harder thing and she has been doing the harder thing since move 11 and she doesn't know how to stop.

*In the shows, the hero delivers the final blow and the music swells and there's a slow-motion sequence and everyone cheers. She delivers the final blow and her nose is bleeding and her ears are bleeding and she can't see out of her right eye and the music isn't swelling because there is no music. There is just a board with most of the pieces gone and a queen standing on d7 and the quiet that comes after the last move of a game no one won.*

---

### POST-CHECKMATE: What remains

**The math:** Six activations. One capture. Cellular baseline at or near transcendence threshold. She is alive. She does not know for how long. The treatment dependency is now existential — without Ruth's CR-7, without Ryu's monitoring, she is a countdown with no display.

**The both/and:** She won the game AND the game cost almost everything. She delivered checkmate AND she is alone on the board. She saved Prime Kain's victims AND she could not save Ruth and Tess from removal. She chose restraint for twenty-four moves AND that restraint meant watching her people fall.

**The worthiness question:** She did the thing only she could do. She hates that it was only her. She hates that the math required everyone else to be spent before she could act. She hates that she was *right* — that staying in the van was correct, that holding c3 while Bellatrix took Ruth and Tess was correct, that being the last piece standing was the optimal outcome.

Being right doesn't feel like being worthy. It feels like being the one who locked up after the party.

**What she carries forward:** The taste of copper. The memory of c4 — Leah's square, her square, the same square. The sound of comms going static when Ruth and Tess were removed. The knowledge that Eidolon's fear-blunder gave her the checkmate and she doesn't know why and she can't control whether it happens again. The cellular cost that will define every decision in every chapter that follows.

And Prime Kain on d7. Static since M8. Removed by the one person he never accounted for. The hermit. The one who stayed in the apartment. The one her own brother eulogized as already dead.

*She's still here. She's not fixed. She's not whole. She's bleeding from places that shouldn't bleed and she can feel the edge of something that isn't human pressing against the inside of her skin.*

*But the game is over. And she's the one who ended it.*
