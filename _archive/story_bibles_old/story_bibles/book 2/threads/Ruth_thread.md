**Climax (Months 11-12):** Coordinates team during manhunt; watches Ahdia burn critically; election loss despite perfect evidence and stopped coup

**End (Book 2):** Can't save everyone proven completely; Ahdia alive but on final reserves; country lost despite everything; processing that effort matters even when outcomes fail

**Key Limitation:** Can't save everyone; work as distraction from feelings; triage thinking as coping mechanism

**Key Costs:** Relationship with Ahdia strained; Firas grief still unprocessed beneath work; watched election fail despite perfect execution
