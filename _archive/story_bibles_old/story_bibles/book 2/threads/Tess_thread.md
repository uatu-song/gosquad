## Arc Summary

**Start (Month 1):** Righteous anger at system that killed Firas; officers' trial pending; relationship with Leta beginning

**Middle (Months 2-7):** Officers cleared by father's protected system; anger channeled into documentation and team work; relationship with Leta deepening despite external pressure

**Father's Betrayal (Months 5-6):** Father explicitly protected officers who killed Firas; chose system over justice; Tess cut ties completely

**Climax (Months 11-12):** Parkour skills save family during manhunt; mission success with team; election loss despite everything

**End (Book 2):** Officers cleared, father's betrayal complete, community knows truth even if courts don't; relationship with Leta survived; "at least we're still here"

**Key Limitation:** Righteous anger sometimes clouds judgment; protective instincts can become controlling

**Key Costs:** Father relationship destroyed; officers who killed Firas protected; system proved to work against them exactly as expected
