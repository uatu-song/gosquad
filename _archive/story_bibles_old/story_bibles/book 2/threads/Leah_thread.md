## Arc Summary

**Start (Month 1):** White moderate; supports goals but uncomfortable with methods; conflict avoidance as coping mechanism

**Education (Month 2):** Victor teaches "but vs and" framework; learns to identify when she's prioritizing comfort over action; commits to noticing her patterns

**Failure (Months 5-7):** Fails to speak up publicly for Leta despite knowing better; uses "but" thinking to justify silence (support her BUT stay safe)

**Action (Month 11):** Releases red dress heist recording; coordinates with 15-20 survivors; makes trauma public; watches it fail within 72 hours

**Climax (Month 11-12):** Hunted for speaking up; roller derby skills save her; recording failed but survivors connected; election lost

**End (Book 2):** "Systemic resistance isn't optional. It's just... constant"; white moderate radicalized through direct experience; did the work, outcome failed, effort still mattered

**Key Limitation:** Conflict avoidance; white moderate comfort prioritization; "but" thinking habit

**Key Costs:** Trauma made public; hunted by mob; recording failed; radicalization complete (can't unsee systemic reality)
