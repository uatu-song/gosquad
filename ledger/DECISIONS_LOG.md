# Decisions Log — the Don't-Relitigate List

Every ruling the author makes, logged the moment he makes it: date, **his words verbatim**,
scope, and rationale. Check this before reopening anything. An agent's gloss of a ruling is
not the ruling (a gloss once over-scoped a ruling and cost 33 sites); quote him.

A sweep or trim claim needs a measured count (`census.py "<pattern>" --since <ref>`): the
pre-commit gate blocks an entry that says "sweep"/"eliminated"/"trim executed" with no number.
