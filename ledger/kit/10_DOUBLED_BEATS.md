# PASS 10 — DOUBLED BEATS AND ONE RENDERING PER BEAT

Read GUARDRAILS.md and COLLABORATOR_RULES.md first.

Two related lenses from Resonance's last revision. Both are reading passes; no script finds them.

## A. Editing artifacts (beats delivered twice)
Readers (one per chapter range, reading every line) look for: a beat announced then performed;
the same reveal or line twice; a scene split or moved that left a copy behind; dialogue repeating
the narration; a name or fact "learned" twice as if new.
For each finding: both quotes (chapter:line), why it looks like an artifact, the MINIMAL fix,
confidence. **Verify every quote against the file before it reaches Joe** (Resonance: 124/124 checked).
Then function-test it: in Resonance a "name revealed twice" was deliberate — the first mention is
under the character's nose and only lands when she sees the shirt. The reader's premise was wrong.
Present as a file with a `MARK:` line per item (Y = apply that fix, N = leave both, or his own line).

## B. One rendering per beat (Joe's rule, 2026-10-17)
"Emotions, the esoteric/abstract, and tone-setting take metaphor; blocking and stage movement take
plain description; default plain; **never both for one beat.** A figure with an antecedent is a paid
setup and outranks the gloss."
Calibration he gave on the first sites — apply it before presenting anything:
- A SINGLE figure on a beat is not a flag, physical or not. The pass is about a beat delivered
  twice (two sentences, or plain sentence + figure sentence) and appositive dashes that restate.
- For each site, classify: is it describing something ABSTRACT or PHYSICAL? Make a guess at the
  ruling from that and state it; he says correct or not. Abstract → the figure survives. Physical →
  plain survives. Fuse rather than cut when both halves carry something.
- Appositive dash restating the noun before it → comma (he delegated these in Resonance once he had
  seen ten; ask before assuming the same here).
Present as a ruling sheet: site · text verbatim · what doubles / what the rule calls for · his
ruling verbatim · result. Narration only. Nothing applied until marked.
