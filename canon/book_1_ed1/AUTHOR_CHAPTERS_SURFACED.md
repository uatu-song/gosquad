## WHAT IS GOOD IN CH08 AND SHOULD SURVIVE ANY REBUILD

Whatever is decided, these are load-bearing and are the author's:

- **`At Ahdia's pyre.`** The closing beat. The whole chapter exists to land it.
- **`And Harding Kain—Kain and his security detail were all tied up. Roped,
  zip-tied, chained.`** followed by **`None of them had been restrained when
  the rocket fired.`** — the clue, delivered without comment. This is the
  chapter's real work: the reader assembles what happened before Ruth does.
- **`She was outside.`** Three words carrying the entire impossibility.
- **`Ruth opened her mouth to speak but no sound would come out.`**
- The withholding itself. Ahdia is never explained, and Ruth's refusal to look
  away from the fire is the whole of her grief with no interiority spent on it.

## THE DECISION IN FRONT OF THE DIRECTOR

Ch08 is 473 narration words — the shortest chapter in the book. Options, in
ascending order of intrusion:

1. **Leave it.** It ships as is. The baseline is untouched.
2. **Surgical: the five voice/adverb tags and the "teleporting" line.** Roughly
   nine words removed, no rewriting. This alone takes ch08 from an outlier to
   inside the author's band on the two worst markers, and removes the one
   phrase that spoils the chapter's own withholding.
3. **Director rewrites it by hand.** 473 words.
4. **Dialogue-frozen rebuild** like ch12–25. Its dialogue is strong and would
   be preserved byte-identical; only narration is regenerated.

**Recommended: 2, then reassess.** It is the smallest intervention that fixes
the measurable problem, it touches no structure, and because ch08 is 473 words
of a 10,830-word baseline, the effect on the measurement ruler is negligible —
whereas a full rebuild of a baseline chapter is not.

**Nothing has been changed. Awaiting per-passage rulings.**
