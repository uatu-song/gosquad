## WORLD RULES AS ESTABLISHED ON-PAGE

- **Caledonia:** third-biggest US city, between twin rivers, 150-year commerce
  hub. Named places you may use: Little Poland (4-block neighborhood, best
  pierogies), Renata's Bakery on Grand, the Natural History Museum on Grand
  Ave, Caledonia Harbor/the docks, the Overlook, midtown (Ahdia's penthouse —
  keycard floor access, only Ahdia and Firas hold cards). Coin nothing else.
- **The team does not drive — ever.** On foot and rooftops, together or on
  comms (handheld ham radios, radio jargon, "five-by-five"). Consumer-grade
  gear: GoPros, AUX cables, binoculars, hydration packs, utility belts, smoke
  grenades, IR/nightvision goggles, black tactical armor, Lycra ski masks.
- **Rules of the squad:** stay secret, stay hidden, never take credit; NO
  GUNS; record police abuse rather than intervene (the rule Tess breaks in
  CH10). Public knows them only as blur and rumor — no faces, no names, no
  known base. The Academy is publicly just a parkour school; it is also the
  covert training ground and recruiting pool. Fan chatter exists online only.
- **Training:** krav maga + judo; choreographed group movement in full
  darkness with goggles+smoke (Firas's signature tactic); quick-change duffels
  stashed around the city; "Elvis has left the building" = scatter, lay low,
  await all-clear (first used CH10).
- **The rally cry** (Firas's invention, from their first job): call — "No
  better place than here." / response — "No better time than now."
- **CR-7:** Ruth's cellular-regeneration therapy. Three years of dismissed
  research; first human use on Firas; ~1.8× healing decaying to ~1.5×; needs
  isotopes and lab synthesis she can't access. It keeps Firas alive but weeks
  from recovery.
- **Timeline anchors (ruled):** the parents vanished 15+ years ago (Montana,
  Glacial National Park; Ahdia ~7, Firas ~5). Firas opened the school years
  later; the Go Squad has run ~4 years. Faraz and Naima Bacchus were ancient
  art/antiquities specialists (NOT engineers) with unexplained military
  connections. The chosen-one/black-hole lore is Faraz's campfire telling:
  powers arrive from the cosmos every few millennia, the bearer burns out and
  dies, a royal guard protects them, one froze armies / made them "never
  arrive."

## CHAPTER MAP (compressed; state changes bolded)

**CH01 — Firas POV, the night before the dinner.** Solo patrol via police
scanner; Little Poland mugging stopped ("This ain't Burger King" — his 64th
engagement); Ruth on comms as Nightingale; rally-cry sign-off; **dinner plans
set for tomorrow.** Establishes: fan hashtags (#GoSquad) named the team over
Firas's objection.

**CH02 — flashback, 15+ years back.** Montana camping trip, first the kids
ever join. Faraz's chosen-one lore; Naima's lesson to Ahdia: *"You don't
always have to fix things for other people"* (series thesis; she helped Firas
with the tent poles in secret). Red-light-green-light; Firas seems frozen
mid-motion (ambiguous). **The parents vanish from the campsite.**

**CH03 — Ahdia POV, the dinner night.** Ahdia introduced: penthouse, snooze
button, dead-parents money. Firas no-shows dinner; blood on her elevator
button; **Firas shot (close-range shotgun through armor), refuses hospital,
demands Ruth.** Ruth extracts fragments, administers **CR-7's first human
dose**, sibling blood transfusion. Hard cut to waiting.

**CH04 — next day.** Firas wakes; 1.8× healing. Ahdia demands answers; Ruth's
long account: how she met Firas (ER visits, the Overlook date, the gang
fight), how the Academy recruited the squad, the codenames, the rules, the
member sketches. **Ahdia now knows everything about the team.**

**CH05 — same day.** Firas's account of his shooting: saw a man abducted from
the Natural History Museum, rode the truck to the docks, watched **Kain**
interrogate the captive over a crate holding an impossible object — visible
from all sides at once ("an object in four dimensions," Ahdia names it).
Discovered, chased, shot mid-dive. CR-7 decaying; **no patrol for a month+.**
Sibling blowout fight; **Ahdia storms out and walks to the docks alone.**

**CH06 — that night, rain.** Ahdia tries to break into the warehouse; **Ruth
appears** (tailed her), reveals casual parkour mastery; they enter through
Firas's broken window. **The crate: diamond logo, "TRIOMF"** — first
appearance. A gunman corners them.

**CH07 — capture and rescue.** Ruth disarms the gunman (broken elbow) but his
shot passed through the crate — **Ahdia is hit in the chest.** Both taken.
Kain in the flesh: beats Ruth, lead pipe theater, "little bird." Ahdia,
bleeding out, dreams of her mother. **The squad rescues Ruth** (smoke +
goggles + choreography). Kain personally fires an RPG into the room.

**CH08 — the impossible.** Everyone — squad, goons — is suddenly OUTSIDE,
unhurt; **Kain and his detail are expertly bound; nobody did it.** The
warehouse burns. **Ahdia is missing; the team believes she died in the fire
("Ahdia's pyre").**

**CH09 — two nights later.** News cycle: Kain spins the "burglary" on Carl
Tucker Tonight; police state context; Chief John Whitford. Ruth patrols
distracted with Battlea + Gloom Girl; grief and confusion about the warehouse
(Gloom Girl: "I've just upped my medication"). A traffic stop below starts
going wrong: mother, six-year-old, guns drawn.

**CH10 — the stop and the chase.** Cops cuff the six-year-old; **Gloom Girl
breaks stand-down orders** and drops one; Battlea takes the other. Helicopter
+ blockades + dogs — a response far too big and fast ("nobody knew we
existed"). "Elvis" scatter. **Gloom Girl, cornered mid-bridge, flips the
police off and swan-dives into the river.**

**CH11 — same night.** Battlea cornered in a construction site, **shot into
her armor** (clavicle bruise); Sable's rebar rescue. On a rooftop, surrounded:
**every police weapon jams; Sable kicks an officer a full block; Battlea
shoulder-charges two through a ledge.** Powers, unmistakably, for the first
time — while Ahdia is "dead." Sable: "I'm a freakin' superhero!"

## KNOWLEDGE STATE AT END OF CH11 (gates — violating these kills a draft)

- **Team believes:** Ahdia died in the warehouse fire. Nobody says otherwise.
- **Team knows:** something impossible saved them in CH08; now their bodies do
  impossible things. They have NO explanation and no suspicion of Ahdia.
- **Firas:** bedridden since CH04; was not present for CH06–11's events;
  whether/how he has been told about Ahdia is UNSTATED — do not stage that
  conversation unless your packet's beats call for it.
- **Nobody knows:** what the 4D object is, what TRIOMF is, who bound Kain,
  why the guns jammed, how the police knew where to be.
- **Reader knows (dramatic irony to preserve):** Ahdia is the anomaly.
- **Author-level (never on the page in this era):** the powers ARE Ahdia,
  amplified and distributed externally — never in-body, never implants;
  Whitford is Tess's father; the object connects to Faraz's chosen-one lore.
