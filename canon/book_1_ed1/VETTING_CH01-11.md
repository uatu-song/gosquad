## Needs ruling before agents read (the leak-class items)

### 1. The codename map in the canon layer is wrong — the prose is unambiguous
`canon/book_1/CONSTRAINTS.yaml:60` and the July CH18 packet both declare
**CRIMSON SABLE=Tess, GLOOM GIRL=Ahdia**. The first edition contradicts this on
multiple pages:
- Sable is **male**: "touched **his** own face" (CH08); "Thanks, **chica**...
  **señorita**" (CH11) → **Sable = Victor**
- Gloom Girl has depression and medication (CH09), is the probable dancer who
  bankrolls the team (CH04), dives off the bridge (CH10) that CH16 later calls
  her first **teleport**, and the CH18 fan roster names her "The 'Porter" →
  **Gloom Girl = Tess**
- **Ahdia has no codename in Book 1** — Gloom Girl exists years before Ahdia
  learns the team exists (CH04), and Ahdia is presumed dead from CH08.

CONSTRAINTS.yaml also asserts "Codenames exist only as fan-invented labels" —
contradicted by CH01 (Ruth is 'Nightingale' on comms) and CH04 ("we all took
codenames... for four years"). The fan account REPEATS the internal names
(Tess leaked them, CH18); it did not invent them.

*If ruled as the prose reads:* CONSTRAINTS.yaml and the July packet get
corrected; the metric packets already carry the correct map.

### 2. The timeline cannot be made to add up
- CH02: the children first join a trip at ages ~7 (Ahdia) and ~5 (Firas); the
  parents vanish ON that trip.
- CH03: the disappearance was "over ten years ago" → Ahdia ≈ 17–18 now.
- CH04: Firas opened his school ~1.5 years after the disappearance → at age
  **~6–7**, impossible; the Go Squad is 4 years old; Ruth (an ER doctor) has
  dated him "for years."
At least one of {Montana ages, "over ten years," school timing} must move.
Nothing in ch1–11 depends on which; the rebuild does.

### 3. Sable's murdered wife vs locked canon
CH04: "Sable had a wife murdered by the gang and that's all we know about him."
Locked canon: **"Victor has NO dead wife (romantic partner is Leah)."** With
Sable=Victor (item 1), the first edition violates the lock directly — and CH11
has Sable/Battlea flirting, which fits the lock and sits oddly beside a
recently murdered wife. Rule: does the line stand for the quarry read, get
excised from the vetted reading copy, or get reassigned?
