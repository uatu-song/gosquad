## END OF BOOK 1
On the page she is alive, in a cloaked CADENS command center on a residential roof, running the team by radio. She told CADENS the Seed is dormant, refused the badge, came back with a duffel, and called Ruth "boss." She believes she was rescued from dying and that her brother died for it; she has told no one — not Ryu, Bourn, or the team beyond "somewhere else" and "a collapsing dimension" — about the corridor or what looked at her. Her last words to Firas were "Firas, please—".
