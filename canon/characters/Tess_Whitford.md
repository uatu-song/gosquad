## END OF BOOK 1
On the page she is on the highest roof at sunset, hood up over a suit CADENS took back that morning, wings on. She got the whole team out of Kain's house, ported Firas off Main Street and came back, said the one sentence the team's rule could not survive and was not there when it landed, and held the end of the chain while Firas went into the point. Her port still works and nobody will tell her why; she wants a new name and has not picked one.

**Page-level, Book 2 inherits:** her father unfaced and the folded photograph on his desk; the drives that prove his payroll; a codename renounced and still worn; a translocation that works without Ahdia; a drone that would not leave her; "We killed them" unprocessed by anyone; the CADENS offer unanswered. **Author-level:** two debts nobody tells her about — the ch22 second and the ch27 sentence (log); her power survives because FAERIS learned Ahdia's job (§7, ch20) and no character may say so; Firas is displaced, not dead (§5b); Leta, Isaiah Bennett, Webb, the vigilante turn and the confrontation with her father are Book 2 — import none of it.
