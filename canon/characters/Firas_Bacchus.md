## END OF BOOK 1
On the page he is gone: he drove at a kaiju, gave his sister his armor, held her hands at the singularity with nothing but skin, put the last dose into her while she was somewhere else, said "Oh hey. I knew you were in there somewhere," looked back once, and "went down into a space smaller than a fist and was absorbed." Every character reads death — Ruth ("We just lost Firas. Yesterday."), Ahdia ("She never got to say goodbye."), the roof ("that's what Firas would've wanted") — and the narration says nothing else. He never learned the powers were his sister's, what they cost her, or that the dose that saved her was one she had kept for him.

Book 2 inherits — **page-level:** a founder mourned by five people who go on in his name ("It was always his"); the talk he owed Ahdia; the estate he spent on Kain's PAC and Victor's lawsuit over it; one sedan nobody has ruled on. **Author-level:** **displaced, not dead** (bible §5b endpoint; log ch29/ch30) — returns in Book 7; never on the page, unknown even to the woman in the tank room; the proposal he meant to make (YAML: Book 2 reveal).
