## END OF BOOK 1
On the page he stands on the highest roof in Caledonia at sunset in a CADENS suit he did not sign for, his old vest over it, four seconds behind Ruth on the wings, having anchored the chain that pulled Ahdia back and watched Firas go into the point. He holds the drives' line — "We have the proof to expose the cops" — and has been told it will stop at the PAC. He told Ahdia "We trusted you" and has not taken it back; he told the room "We don't. We keep up the fight." His powers have been gone since the docks.
