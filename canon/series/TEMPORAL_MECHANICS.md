# TEMPORAL MECHANICS — the bible

**Why this exists (Director, 2026-08-29, reading ch15):** "The rules are
accumulating — pillows obey her private physics, electricity doesn't, touched
objects carry stored force, now fabric shear — and they're one contradiction
away from needing arbitration. Write the bible before ch16, not after."

**What this is:** every rule of Ahdia's temporal power as established on the
page in the metric drafts (ch12–15) and the vetted corpus, each with its
source. Packets cite this file; agents obey it; contradictions between a beat
and this file are DEAD DRAFTS. Open questions at the bottom are exactly that —
unruled; agents must not resolve them.

---

## 1. The trigger

- **Emotion pulls the trigger. Calm releases it.** Frustration/anger/panic
  starts a freeze; genuine calm — or the hollowed-out surrender under panic —
  ends it. Commands, gestures, and wanting do nothing. (14a; 14b beat 1.)
- Release by surrender: when command fails, *please* works. (14a, the Firas
  fake-out.)
- The hazard is structural: a device keyed to panic and unlocked by serenity,
  issued to a nervous system already running hot. Any future startle is armed.
  (14a, ruled register.)
- With practice she can enter at will (getting angry on purpose) and hold
  deliberately; releases become less accidental. Seventeen freezes on day
  one; thirty-minute holds by day three. (14b beats 1–3.)

## 2. The field

- The freeze is a BUBBLE with an edge — "a bridal train made of stopped
  clocks." A thing with an edge has a size; a thing with a size can shrink.
  (14a stairwell.)
- Shrinking the field cuts cost: −30% on first attempt, half on the second.
  Control halves the burn. (ch15 training room.)
- **Selective dilation:** the field can be aimed INSIDE her own body — slow
  the burn in her legs, and only there. Previous Temporalists took 12–47
  years to learn this; she did it in under an hour. (14b bridge; FAERIS.)
- Aiming it at her own HEART nearly killed her — the pulse comes back with
  interest, every postponed beat delivered at once. Muscles yes, engine no.
  (14a; 14b remembers it as "how narrowly that trick had missed being her
  last idea.")
- Unconscious operation is possible: 23 continuous minutes, ~6.1 m radius,
  while sprinting, without knowing. (14a, FAERIS report.)

## 3. Objects and force

- Inside the freeze, objects she handles obey HER physics — thrown pillows
  fly and land normally. On release, a gently-moved object simply IS
  elsewhere, no history of travel. (14b beat 2.)
- **Force amplification:** motion imparted and released mid-flight comes out
  enormous — a tossed pillow fires "out of something military"; a fingertip
  push arrives as a shove from something far bigger. Dosable with practice:
  tap → thump → launch. 47 amplifications logged at the ambush. (14b; FAERIS.)
- People can be carried, repositioned, set down mid-air; they experience
  nothing and arrive with the story their eyes invent (Gloom Girl
  "teleported"). (14b bridge; ch10 vetted.)

## 4. What stops and what doesn't

- **Ordinary electricity does not function inside the field.** Elevators are
  boxes of electricity; the stairwell was dark because emergency lights are
  electricity too. (14a, ruled staging.)
- **CADENS field-hardened gear is the exception**, engineered like the suit:
  the HUD, the panic watch, FAERIS units. On the page via Ryu: "ordinary
  electronics stop dead inside your bubble, which is how I ended up watching
  you climb thirty-four floors instead of taking the elevator." (ch15.)
- The HUD's AR runs at picosecond framerates INSIDE the field; AR-Ryu is a
  cybernetic mapping who knows only what real-Ryu knew at the freeze, and
  real-Ryu receives the conversation only at release. Both directions hard.
  (ch15; locked canon.)
- **Temporal shear degrades ordinary fabric** with exposure — her hoodie
  cuffs furred "like rope just before it parts" after ~six frozen hours,
  discovered on the page in 14b, explained in ch15. The suit's fabric is
  rated for it. (14b retrofit; ch15.)

## 5. The cost

- Every use spends a finite reserve that DOES NOT recharge — permanent
  cellular depletion, not fatigue. (14b, FAERIS bio-sign verdict.)
- The ledger (Director-ruled, the meter reconciliation): **eighteen months is
  the BASELINE figure** — short freezes, small corrections, nothing heroic.
  The ambush night was capital, not rate: 23% of total reserves in one
  night; "four more like it and we are not having this conversation." (14b.)
- Control extends runway: halving the burn "bought yourself another year.
  Maybe more." (ch15.)
- The Seed is **COLD, always** — ice, never heat. Cost registers as cold
  crossing into pain, then as emptying out. (Everywhere; hard lock.)
- The body pays real bills inside frozen time: sweat, muscle fatigue, hunger,
  exhaustion — "the universe bills effort somewhere no matter what the
  muscles agreed to." (14b.)
- The Seed is REPLACING her: threads through cardiovascular, nervous,
  skeletal systems, converting tissue into something that channels temporal
  energy. Converted tissue is still her, still functions, just different.
  Endpoint at baseline: more Seed than human. (ch15 med bay.)

## 6. Adjacent tech (not her power)

- **Translocation** is CADENS/FAERIS machinery (the paint, the pull, space
  folding), not a Seed ability. Vomit has a protocol. (ch13–14a.)
- The team's "powers" in Book 1 are HER, distributed invisibly — the wall
  between that truth and the Go Squad is the series engine and is never
  weakened. (Her ruled conditions, ch15.)

   2:47 stays 2:47). Possibilities (leakage between chained freezes; imperfect
   holds) are NOT established. The line stands as her observation only.

   — never shown. Do not stage it either way without a ruling.
3. **FAERIS units inside vs outside her field** during observations — hardened
   (they function near her freezes) but their in-field behavior is unstaged.
4. **Whether hunger/aging inside long holds accumulate measurably** — implied
   by the sweat rule, never quantified. Do not quantify.
