## 2026-09-07 — Synopsis punch #16 RULED: translocation is FAERIS, not Ahdia

**Director, verbatim:** "Ahdia doesn't translocate herself--it's a FAERIS
function, standard since book 1."

**So:** translocation is TECH. FAERIS moves people — Tess in Book 1 (ch21
"FAERIS drones doing the actual teleporting"; ch23 "Drones." "FAERIS."
"Yes."), Ahdia in 2A (the Eidolon-mob extraction, Jakarta, every global op),
the team in the endgame. **It costs no baseline.** Ahdia's power stays
temporal: freeze, dilation, the acceleration field. The 2B evaluation's
"translocation mechanics" issue (#3) is closed by this.

**Consequences for the synopsis (crew, derived — veto open):**
1. Month 1's "professional sniper, translocation signature" on Geneva
   Windrow now reads as a TECH signature: either FAERIS itself was used (who
   has access?) or someone fields FAERIS-class displacement. Unruled which;
   it is the hidden-orchestrator layer either way (Geneva is Bellatrix's
   avatar in series canon).
2. Month 12's "Translocation: Ahdia moves team 800 miles (3min lifespan
   cost, baseline 10% → 7%)" is wrong on its face — the jump is FAERIS's and
   free; the freeze is the burn. 2B's synopsis rewrites the cost line.
3. "Ahdia translocates to safety" (Month 1) → FAERIS extracts her. Her
   global reach in 2A is FAERIS's reach.

**Ruled by:** Director. Consequences crew, veto open.

## 2026-09-07 — Synopsis punch #12 RULED: the Seed was never dormant

**Director, verbatim:** "Never dormant but I think this could be more clearly
illustrated; the secret from CADENS but not the team. So, for the reader
experience, the first use of the powers should also be a bit of a surprise
(both if they read book 1 and if they started with book 2)."

**What this makes of Book 1's page (no touch needed):** ch30 line 91 —
"I can't use my powers anymore. The Seed's dormant. Maybe permanently." — is
**Ahdia lying to Bourn**, frozen dialogue, as the badge crosses the desk. She
takes the badge (the epilogue's "Agent Auerbach"). CADENS believes it (Bourn,
ch30: "Track the Seed-bearers. And hope that next time, a depressed girl with
time powers and a death wish isn't our only line of defense"); the opposition
believes it (epilogue: "The plan was always to eliminate her once the Seed
went dormant" — suspended). **The team is never shown believing it.** The lie
is her cover for everything 2A hides.

**What this requires of 2A (crew reading of "more clearly illustrated" —
veto open):** the illustration is in 2A, not Book 1, because a Book 1 tell
would spend the surprise. 2A withholds the power from the reader until the
riot freeze in Month 1; **the team's non-surprise at the freeze is how the
reader learns the team knew and CADENS did not.** POV control: no team-POV
line before the riot may reference her power as live.

**The number:** the synopsis's "Baseline: 100%" is struck. 2A opens from
Book 1's **one-third cellular recovery** plus ~three months of treatment,
derived by whatever decay model punch #20 rules. Nobody starts at 100%.

**Ruled by:** Director (never dormant; secret from CADENS not the team;
first use a surprise for both readers). Crew: where the illustration lives,
the number's derivation. Veto open on both.
