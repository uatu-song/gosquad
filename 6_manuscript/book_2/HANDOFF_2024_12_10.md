## STRUCTURAL DISCUSSION - BOOK 2A vs 2B PACING

### Concern Addressed:
Book 2A (Months 1-7) might feel like "navel-gazing" compared to Book 1's action.

### Resolution:
2A is tension-building, not lesser. This is the series form working correctly:

- **Book 1:** Action drives emotion (hook readers, prove you can deliver)
- **Book 2A:** Emotion builds tension (pot boiling, slow burn)
- **Book 2B:** Tension explodes into action (everything detonates)
- **Book 3:** High octane payoff (foundation from 2A/2B rewarded)

Readers who trust Book 1 will follow through 2A's slow burn to get 2B's detonation.

### 2A Action Beats (sufficient):
- Month 1.03: Isaiah riot
- Month 1.08: Geneva assassination
- Month 2.08: Victor attacked by fear-manipulated civilians
- Month 6.10: Gala infiltration (Tess/Korede)
- Month 7.10-7.20: Confrontation + Exile Island reveal

The gap in Months 3-5 is intentional tension-building, not a flaw.
