**THE CHAPTER'S REAL ASSIGNMENT.** Ahdia has thirty percent of a body and the
whole street. Three jobs:

1. **She wins by moving people, not by hitting them.** Every victory in this
   chapter is repositioning inside a stop: the Tanks are turned into each
   other, Kain's fist is dodged by walking, Philips's gun is emptied and put
   back in his hand. She never lands a blow. That is the character and it is
   the book's argument.
2. **The comedy is the armor and it is failing.** She jokes continuously —
   pad thai, action-movie banter, hospital chic — and the jokes get thinner as
   the blood loss deepens. Never explain a joke. Never let one soften a
   symptom. Both are true at once.
3. **FIRAS DOES NOT KNOW SHE IS ALIVE.** He drove at a kaiju because his team
   was dying, not because he knew his sister lived. **His frozen line is now
   just "Hey, Ahdia."** — the smallest thing a man can say when the biggest
   thing will not fit. Play the recognition; do NOT have anyone explain how
   she is alive, and do NOT let him arrive already knowing.

**REGISTER:** Ahdia POV throughout. Pop-culture cognition is her operating
system and belongs here in force (§7g: register follows POV, and this POV is
the funny one). Her interiority runs on external observation — other people's
hands, posture, the state of the street — because that is how she avoids
looking at herself.

## BEATS (order fixed; texture yours; no scene breaks)

1. **Cold feet, and the inventory.** Barefoot on filthy asphalt, hospital gown,
   blood onto pale blue fabric. She reads her own situation the way she reads
   everything — from outside, as if it were happening to someone on a screen.
2. **Kain at twenty-three feet.** Translucent skin, the Heart in his sternum,
   greenish light through the torso, bones audibly restructuring. **He is still
   growing during the chapter.** Behind him the four Tanks — **Ogden** (scar
   through the left eyebrow), **Philips** (thick neck; the one who strangled
   Ruth during the thirty minutes before Ahdia arrived — this is where Ruth's
   bruised throat in ch24–25 came from), **Edgar** (bald, knuckles already
   bloody), **Orlansky** (youngest, eyes too wide). She knows their names from
   a CADENS briefing Ryu showed her. **Do not invent a fifth.**
3. **The banter.** Kain's contempt, her deflection, the pad thai riff, the
   action-movie riff. He tells her she is no hero and **she agrees, twice, and
   means it.** This is the chapter's quiet center: she is not being modest.
4. **What she sees while stalling** — the team in position on rooftops and fire
   escapes: Gloom Girl on the bank, Battlea behind the overturned bus, Night
   Knight with smoke, Crimson Sable with his staff, Ruth coordinating.
   **REQUIRED, Director-assigned:** Ahdia registers, from the outside and
   without understanding it, that **the team is taking Ruth's orders without
   looking at her.** She does not know what she is seeing. One beat, no more,
   and nobody explains it.
5. **The car.** A car coming fast down Main Street — **Firas, in his own
   civilian car, out of costume.** (§7b permits this: it is a private act by a
   benched man, not team transport. The team still owns no vehicle.) Her
   reaction is the warm painful thing that is not the Seed: **he came.** Play
   it against ch12 — he stood over what he thought was her body and said she
   was already dead inside.
6. **The Tanks rush, and she turns them into each other.** Stop, walk between
   them, adjust angles, step clear, release. They collide. **A Saturday-morning
   cartoon is the right reference for how she thinks about it.**
7. **Kain's fist, dodged by walking.** Stop, walk ten or fifteen feet out of
   the strike path, release. He craters the pavement and stumbles.
8. **Philips's gun.** Stop, take it, drop the magazine, put the empty weapon
   back in his hand and fix his grip, step clear, release. Click. Click.
   **DO NOT HAVE HER COUNT THE BULLETS OR ANYTHING ELSE — see the ban below.**
9. **The collapse, and the grab.** Her body quits: grey edges, hands on knees,
   drowning in air. Kain closes instead of swinging, takes her around the waist
   and squeezes. Ribs creak, then something cracks. She cannot reach the Seed.
10. **The timing problem, and the push.** She has ONE shot: too early and Firas
    hits a frozen Kain at speed and dies; too late and she is crushed. She
    holds until the car is on him, gets out spans 31–32 with no air, and
    **pushes — RED, not blue, forward not back** — accelerating herself and
    Firas inside a world stopped at the moment of impact. She pulls him into
    her timestream; he tumbles out of the car onto frozen pavement.
11. **The two of them on the ground.** The frozen tableau: Kain's face
    mid-realisation, the Tanks scattered, the hood crumpled against his shin,
    steam and glass hanging. Then **"Hey, Ahdia." / "Hey, little bro."** — two
    people saying the smallest available thing. He assesses her injuries the
    way Ruth would. His apology, and **her answer is "I know," NOT "it's
    okay,"** because it is not okay: the words at the funeral landed like
    bullets and confirmed everything she believes about herself. And he came
    anyway. They agree to talk later. **Do not resolve it here.**
12. **THE SUIT — Director-framed (move map stud 6). Do not improvise this.**
    He strips down to the Go Squad armour worn under his clothes out of habit
    and holds it out to her. From his side this is not a costume change and it
    is not powers: it is his armour and the closest thing he has to a name, and
    **he fumbles it** — reaches for something dignified and lands on a joke
    about the shoulders. He thinks he is lending it. He thinks he is getting it
    back. What he has actually done is put his shell on his sister and step
    aside: the protector reflex finding the one form he cannot outrun. He does
    not know that, and the narration must NOT say it.
    **Then the staged image, which is the beat:** she holds the suit up, moves
    to slam it to the ground — and in that instant **she is already wearing
    it.** Powers implied, never stated. No freeze is described, no cost is
    named, nothing is explained. **Too big in the shoulders. Still barefoot.**
    A quiet frozen-time lull before the final march.
13. **The signal.** She asks about the team, he answers, he takes cover across
    the street with no armour and absolute faith. She stands in the centre of
    the intersection and shouts the rally cry, and the Go Squad comes down off
    the roofs screaming it back. **END THERE.**

## WORLD + CANON LOCKS (violating any = dead draft)

- **NOBODY COUNTS. EVER.** No character counts, tallies, or does arithmetic
  about anything — not bullets, not seconds, not floors, not their own life.
  Also banned: arithmetic, the ledger, came due, an accounting, the bill, the
  exchange rate used figuratively. This includes Ahdia. It is a house tic, not
  a trait. **The gun is emptied without a count.**
- **§7g NAMING.** Ahdia's POV, so the team are **codenames** — Night Knight,
  Battlea, Gloom Girl, Crimson Sable — because she has never met them. **Firas
  and Ruth are names**, because she knows them.
- **§7b THE VEHICLE.** Firas's car is a private civilian act, ruled permitted.
  The team still owns no vehicle; nobody else drives anything.
- **§7d TIMELINE.** She collapsed at the docks LAST NIGHT and was unconscious
  eighteen hours. Ryu has been treating her for about a day — **never "two
  days"**. Late September, and the book is undated.
- **§5b COST.** Monotonic decline. Every stop costs more than the last, and
  she is at ~30% regeneration. Red is push, blue is pull; mark the difference,
  never explain the mechanism.
- Four Tanks, named as above. **No fifth.** Kain is twenty-three feet and
  growing. The Heart is in his sternum.
- **Firas does not know she is alive until he sees her. Nobody explains how.**
- **FIRAS NEVER LEARNS ABOUT THE POWERS** (`Firas_run1.md`, ~M116–120: "he
  doesn't know about powers, he never will"). He can see that something
  impossible is happening around him in this chapter — he is standing in a
  stopped street — but **he never learns that his sister was the source of the

  Do not let him work it out on the page.
- **Ahdia withholds the cost** (`Ahdia_run1.md`, M110). The team may know it is
  her; they may NOT know it is killing her. She gives them the power and keeps
  the ledger. That line holds through this chapter — she does not tell Firas
  what any of this is costing her, and the narration does not tell him either.
