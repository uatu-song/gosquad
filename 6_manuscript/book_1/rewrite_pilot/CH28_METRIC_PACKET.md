## BEATS (order fixed; texture yours; no scene breaks)

1. **Her legs go.** One knee, one hand on cold pavement, the suit hanging
   heavy, lungs burning, vision going at the edges. **The four husks.**
   Ogden, Philips, Edgar, Orlansky, empty where men were. **And here, once:
   it was Gloom Girl who said it, and Gloom Girl is not here any more.**
2. **Kain at forty feet and still growing**, bones cracking like gunshots,
   the Heart pulsing in his chest like a dying star.
3. **Ruth calls the move; Kain moves first.** The fist comes down like a
   building falling. **Ahdia reaches for the Seed and nothing happens.** Too
   depleted — she spent it getting them here, surviving the impact, changing
   clothes in a blink.
4. **Battlea shoves her — she is LEAH from here.** The fist craters where
   Ahdia was kneeling; cracks twenty feet out, shrapnel, a chunk of asphalt
   the size of a basketball past her head.
5. **Scatter.** Night Knight's command. Leah left, fastest, drawing him.
   Crimson Sable right. Night Knight to tactical position. Ruth hauls Ahdia
   up and asks if she can run; **Ahdia nods and lies.**
6. **Kain grabs at Leah and closes on air** — she changed direction, derby
   instinct, reading momentum and taking the angle.
7. **Crimson Sable's rebar off Kain's ankle** — it should have shattered bone
   and it bounces like concrete, the shock going up his arms. His line cracks
   with grief in it. **Then the casual backhand, and he hits a building thirty
   feet away and does not move. He is VICTOR from here.**
8. **Leah runs the wrong way** — toward him, not away — and Ruth screams her
   name. The fist comes down and Leah dives, rolls, comes up running, reaches
   him. He is breathing. He says he is okay and does not finish it.
9. **Night Knight tackles them both clear**, not Kain — full-body, out of the
   grab radius, all three tumbling while the fingers close on nothing.
10. **Kain's taunt.** Spans 14–15, thunder. **"You fed me. Made me whole."**
    Ahdia hears the sentence land on the people it is about.
11. **She tries again and cannot.** Body will not answer. Thirty percent is
    not enough for this. The Seed flickers, responds barely, and it is **not
    enough** — Ruth running, not going to make it, none of them going to make
    it, the hands starting down.
12. **THE SKY SCREAMS.** Displaced air, something faster than sound. **The
    cruise missile into Kain's chest.** White, blinding, a small sun over
    downtown; the wave shatters windows three blocks out. Forty feet of him
    hits the street like a falling building.
13. **CADENS.** Boots, dust, twenty in coordinated formation. **MACK leads
    them** — Ahdia knows him from the facility. He reports to **Bourn**, who
    answers in his earpiece loud enough for Ahdia to hear. **"Confirmed. Kain
    is dead."**
14. **What the missile cost somebody.** Mack's flat account: Overseer wanted
    authorization, the Pentagon wanted assurances, SecDef wanted
    contingencies, and they stopped waiting. **"Some things are more important
    than careers, ma'am."**
15. **The false ending.** Ahdia on the pavement, legs splayed, chest heaving,
    staring at the corpse. **Dead. They'd won.** Ruth waves off a CADENS medic
    because she is a doctor and she has this. Ruth asks after Firas and Gloom
    Girl; they are safe, translocated out before engagement.
16. **The ground starts.** Not the missile — smaller, rhythmic, **like a
    heartbeat**, out of the crater. Kain's body moves without being alive.
    The chest caves first, everything compressing toward the Heart.
17. **The collapse.** Forty feet to thirty to twenty. Arms into the torso.
    The temperature drops and she can see her breath. Fifteen, ten, five, and
    then a point no bigger than a basketball two feet above the crater,
    spinning, impossibly dense. **Then it starts pulling.**
18. **The pull.** Debris first, then everything, accelerating and vanishing.
    Ahdia feels it on her body; the oversized suit ripples in a wind that is
    not wind; her hair goes forward. **Ruth is pulling her the other way and
    losing, inch by inch.**
19. **END ON THE BANK BUILDING.** Leaning. Imperceptible, then visible.
    Windows going. Structure groaning. **It is going to fall, and when it does
    it takes everyone.** Stop there.
