exchange rate. Victor's questions land because they are correct. Do NOT let

## BEATS (order fixed; texture yours; # between scenes)

1. **The transport.** The team files up the ramp — Tess dripping, Leah walking
   on her own in the ruined dress. **Firas stays at the extraction point with
   Jericho and the drives** (bible §7b: the team owns no vehicle; he is on
   foot and wounded and can only hold a position). The look he gives Ruth, and
   her honest answer to herself: she is not sure about this. Then the reveal,
   delivered flat because there is no gentle version — the powers were never
   theirs. Watch each of them take it differently: Ben goes pale (he *built*
   tactics around a lie), Victor bitter, Tess quiet, Leah asking the practical
   question. Ruth gives them CADENS, the drones, the transformation, and
   **withholds exactly one thing.**
2. **HAZARD's report and the map.** Kain mobile, northeast, razing block by
   block. "Nowhere. Everywhere."
3. **(#) The extraction point.** Jericho against the dock wall, zip-tied and
   terrified, and Ruth in full gear working him — the file that proves he kept
   working *enthusiastically* after the kidnapping, and the threat that breaks
   him, which is not violence but Kain waking up. This is Ruth at her hardest
   and it should be uncomfortable; she has been awake too long and is spending
   parts of herself she does not usually spend.
4. **(#) The prognosis — PLAIN STRETCH.** Jericho on the Heart: organic, a
   seed from something vast, integration irreversible, Kain being consumed
   rather than empowered. The numbers are frozen: fifteen feet and growing,
   twenty, hours to live, twelve at most, sixty percent odds he completes
   before he dies. Then the answer nobody wants: only another Temporalist can
   stop him. **Ben works it out in one step** and asks the question Ruth
   cannot answer.
5. **The interrogation of Ruth.** Victor's disbelief, then his three
   questions in sequence — you know them / you're not telling us because they
   asked / even though we have a right to know — and Ruth says nothing, which
   is the worst possible answer and the only honest one. Then Leah's question
   about how long the person has, which Ruth answers, and the room goes quiet.
   Ben's refusal ("we can't ask someone to die for us") and Ruth's correction
   ("it's not your choice, it's theirs") — the book's thesis running the other
   direction, from Ruth to the team.
6. **(#) The facility, and Bourn.** Ahdia is awake, has seen the feeds, and is
   **already asking when she can deploy** — which lands on Ruth like a
   diagnosis. Ryu's estimate: days, maybe a week. Ruth asks for ten minutes,
   and the narration must not say what she wants them for.
7. **(#) The briefing room.** Mission-control scale, global threat boards,
   personnel files with their real names on them. Each of them reacts in
   character (Tess turning in place, Ben reading the meta-human tracking,
   Victor finding his own file, Leah taking a chair that adjusts to her). The
   offer, the freedom to walk, the four cases and what each suit holds.
8. **The alarm.** Downtown. Multiple cosmic signatures. And on the wall: Kain
   at twenty feet and still growing, the Heart visible through translucent
   skin — **and behind him the four Tanks**, seven feet of torn CCPD uniform
   each. CADENS deploying by the book, minutes away, while civilians die on
   the feed in real time. The team decides in four looks and no discussion,
   and the frozen lines do the rest. Ruth suits up with them; Bourn does not
   stop her. **END on Bourn alone in the briefing room watching five people
   run at it.**
