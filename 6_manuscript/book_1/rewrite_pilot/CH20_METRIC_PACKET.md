**THE THESIS.** The series runs on one idea — *you don't have to be fixed to
be worthy* — and this chapter is where it gets said out loud for the first
time, in Ruth's frozen dialogue: worth saving "not because you're useful,"
and "you think that makes you useful instead of worthy." Everything in the
first half of the chapter (the lab, the drone, the synthesis) is the setup
that earns it. **The narration must not explain the theme, ever.** No
summarizing what the exchange means, no closing epigram that names it. The
frozen dialogue carries it; the narration stays out of the way. This is the
chapter where an over-eager register would do the most damage.

**REGISTER:** Ruth's half runs clinical and forward-moving — competence
finally given resources, which is its own kind of joy, and the only place in
this chapter where wit belongs (Ryu's ramble, "We don't wait for grant
approval"). Ahdia's half is the book's deepest PLAIN STRETCH: she is at her
lowest and the prose goes flat and stays flat. No jokes at all after she
wakes. Her self-description ("a tool that wears skin") is hers, not the
narration's — do not decorate around it.

   he means.

   odds. The condition that damns it: this only works if Ahdia stops. "Good

   Ruth's answer, which is the chapter's other thesis: they can synthesize
   anything, but dosage judgement is art, learned on patients nobody

   cosmic threats, dimensional breaches, and the hope that FAERIS learns

3. **(#) The detour — the drone.** The observation window: a test chamber, a

   eighteen months. Ruth reaches the implication before he says it. Then the
   word "sentient," which Ryu declines to use yet. Bourn's recruitment
   promise quoted back — help until FAERIS learns, then step back — and the
   thing everyone knows: Ahdia can't step back. "Would you?" and Ruth's
   honest no, routed through her own ER history. Close on the drone

   fingers speed up. Play it cold and small; do not editorialize about AI.
4. **(#) Ahdia wakes — THE PLAIN STRETCH.** Disorientation first: white
   ceiling, wrong equipment, no windows, memory returning in pieces. Then
   Ruth with the case. "I'm alive?" / "For now." The question that opens the

   argument that she is only her usefulness: the apartment, the twelve hours

   just taking up space." Ruth's counter is the thesis and it must be

   achievement — reach it, don't beat it). The thanks. And the last line
   before she leaves: don't tell him. Ruth doesn't ask who. END on Ahdia
   alone with the monitors and the possibility.
