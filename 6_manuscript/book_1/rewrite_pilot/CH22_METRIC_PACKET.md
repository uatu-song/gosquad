## BEATS (order fixed; texture yours; # only where the source breaks)

1. **The office, overwhelmed.** Open fire. Battlea uses zip-tied Kain in the
   wheeled chair as a shield the guards cannot shoot past. Ben behind the
   desk with Victor already opening the catering cart's hidden compartment —
   the loadout ch21 established, paying off. Tess on comms from the parking
   structure: she could pull them out one at a time, but Jericho is
   unconscious in the side room and the copy has thirty seconds left. She
   makes them hold. Smoke goes up; thermal goggles go on; a dozen guards go
   blind and the team does not.
2. **The blind fight.** The chair shoved into the firing line; Battlea taking
   it apart and keeping two armrests as batons. Ben and Victor working the
   smoke from opposite directions. The two of them back to back in the
   middle of it. Then Ben's flying kick puts a guard through the window —
   and **the broken window drafts the smoke out faster than they wanted**,
   which is the turn: their advantage was weather, and they just broke it.
3. **Losing.** Victor headbutted and exposed. Fresh guards, not blinded.
   Battlea takes a hostage and a rifle and calls for Gale — and Ben looks for
   a heat signature that is not there. **The realization lands as three
   short beats: he scans, he does not find her, he remembers where she is.**
   Ruth is at CADENS. THE TEAM DOES NOT KNOW WHY — they know only that she is
   gone and not answering (ch21). Then Ben pinned with a knife over him,
   Victor cornered, Battlea's hostage about to break. They are losing.
4. **The blackout.** The lights die — all of them, and the moonlight seems to
   dim with them. One second of total dark. Tess had ported to the basement
   electrical box and thrown the breakers, which she does NOT explain; the
   prose reveals it as she arrives. She appears eight feet up on the crown
   molding, thermal goggles on, reading the room from above like a route.
   **This is her chapter and this is the moment she becomes the best of
   them.** Then the sequence: the drop onto the knife guard, Ben ported
   across the room, the chandelier swing, Battlea ported to the desk.
   Jericho already gone — ported to the extraction point during the dark.
5. **The window.** Lights back on emergency power. A dozen guards around the
   desk. Tess reads distances and firing lines and finds the answer nobody
   else would: not out through them, but DOWN. She takes Ben and Battlea
   through the broken window, and mid-fall she ports back for Victor, and
   then **uses translocation the way parkour uses walls** — not arresting
   the fall but steering it, jump by jump, until four people who should hit
   concrete enter the fountain at an angle. That principle is the payoff of
   everything the book has said about her: she was always a traceur first.
6. **The alley.** Three blocks east. Victor soaking and baffled. Battlea
   laughing at the ruined dress. "That was fun." Firas arrives ON FOOT at the
   alley mouth — slower than he has ever moved, one hand flat on his ribs
   (bible §7b: no team vehicle). Jericho is at the extraction point,
   unconscious but breathing, with the research.
7. **The neighbor.** A woman in her sixties, bathrobe and slippers, asks if
   they are the Go Squad — and asks for a photo because her nephew is alive.
   The team freezes; Battlea walks over soaking wet in a destroyed dress and
   lets her take it. "We see you. All of us. We see you." **Play this
   completely straight.** It is the last uncomplicated moment in the book and
   it must not be undercut by a joke or a knowing narration beat.
8. **The transformation.** Then light — red and gold, dimensional rather
   than physical, space folding, felt in the bones like translocation
   inverted. Kain walks OUT THROUGH the mansion wall as though it were not
   there. The Heart is embedded in his chest. His eyes flicker between
   states. He looks at the sky, then at the team three blocks away, and
   smiles, and walks off through a building. No fight, no speech: he is
   simply past them now.
9. **The call.** Tess reaching for a phone and having nobody to call — the
   cops work for him. Then her phone rings, unknown number: Ruth, opening
   with the rally cry and not waiting for the response. **Tess's reaction to
   hearing it is the emotional hinge of the ending** — the frozen line about
   wanting to change her codename is hers and must land without explanation.
   Then the VTOL, cloaked until directly overhead, the ramp, and Ruth in full
   tactical gear asking if they need a ride. END there.
