## THE FOUR THINGS THIS CHAPTER MUST GET RIGHT

**1. THE DOSE SHE PALMED (Director-ruled).** In ch24 she took seven doses and
**faked the eighth, keeping it.** She has been carrying it since the medical
bay. She never explained it and does not explain it now. When she reaches for
it here, **she is reaching for it to give to FIRAS** — the whole point of
having kept it was that it was never for her. **She does not manage to.** Her
hand is dissolving; the injector exists in too many dimensions at once. Then
she is gone, and while she is gone **he takes it and puts it in her.**
That inversion is the chapter. Do not editorialise it.

**2. THE ENDPOINT RULE (§5b, absolute).** What happens to her is
**metamorphosis, and every character on the page reads it as death.** Firas
dissolves **believing he is dead and believing it worked.** Ahdia comes back
believing she was rescued from dying. **The narration NEVER says transcendence,
never says ascension, never hints he survives.** The reader gets it only
through the conversion language the chapter already uses — light where flesh
should be, structure visible, a body that is becoming something. **No
character speculates. The narration does not editorialise the door.**

**3. THE ENTITY IS NOT NAMED.** Something vast turns its attention on her in
the hallway and they recognise each other. **Ahdia does not know what it is and
has never heard its name, so no name appears** — not in dialogue, not in
narration. It looks, it acknowledges, it turns away. Dismissal or permission,
and she cannot tell which. **Write the encounter and withhold the noun.**

**4. NAMING — the payoff of the ch28 ruling.** Naming tracks CONTACT and has
been drifting all climax. Ruth and Firas are always names. **Leah** and
**Victor** are already names (ch28). Ben is still **Night Knight** and Tess is
still **Gloom Girl** when this chapter opens — **and then the human chain
forms, and every one of them has hands on her.** At the chain, and only there,
they become **Ben** and **Tess**. That is the last gap closing, and it closes
because they physically refuse to let go of her. **The narration never remarks
on it.**

## BEATS (order fixed; texture yours; no scene breaks)

1. **The pull, escalating.** Gentle first — hair, the loose suit. Then debris
   crossing pavement toward the point where Kain compressed, spinning two feet
   above the crater. Then her own body. Mack screaming the fall-back; CADENS
   moving like people trained for it.
2. **The bank goes.** Ruth grabbing her arm. The structure leaning, windows
   cascading, steel making a sound that is wrong. **Everyone on the street is
   underneath it.**
3. **She sends Ruth away.** Spans 4–8. She shoves her — hard. She can see all
   of them in the radius: Leah getting Victor up, Night Knight favouring his
   ribs, Gloom Girl by the overturned bus.
4. **She walks at it.** Ruth understanding, lunging, Leah too, and **the pull
   keeps them off her.** Each step harder. Bare feet scraping. Ruth screaming
   her name and the word DARE.
5. **Ten feet out, she plants.** Pavement cracking under her. **Arms wide:
   right hand at the singularity, left hand back at the city.**
6. **RED from the right hand.** The push. Aimed into the core — burn it out,
   get it to its end faster. **Not enough.**
7. **BLUE from the left.** The pull, as a shell around the acceleration, to
   keep it from spreading. **Both directions running through one body at once.**
8. **What that costs, immediately.** Her skin cracks — literally, fissures up
   the arms and across the chest, and what shows through is not blood. She
   screams. **The Seed can do this; she is only the conduit, and the conduit is
   what tears.** Her vision fragments; the hallway starts bleeding in at the
   edges and she refuses it.
9. **FIRAS.** Hands over hers, warm and human and real, and she knows before
   she can turn. Span 13. **He has no Seed. He is only flesh.** His hands crack
   the same way hers do. Spans 14–19 — she tries to make him let go and he
   will not, and **his voice is calm because he has already decided.**
10. **She reaches for the dose — FOR HIM.** The one she palmed in ch24. She
    gets it free. **She cannot deliver it.** Her hand is going; she can see
    through her own fingers; the injector is falling and floating and existing
    in too many places. Span 20 is her trying to say his name.
11. **THE HALLWAY.** Not bleeding in any more — complete. Infinite corridor,
    doors past counting in both directions, each one lit differently. She looks
    at herself and finds light instead of flesh, structure and depth and time
    visible, past versions overlapping the present. **No mouth, no lungs. Just
    awareness.** The doors are timelines: a life where she never left the
    apartment, one where she died at the warehouse, **one where Firas lives and
    she does not.**
12. **Something looks back.** From the end of the corridor and directly in
    front of her at once. Not human, never human. **Recognition, both ways, no
    words.** It acknowledges her and turns its attention elsewhere. She has
    questions and gets none of them answered. **NAME NOTHING.**
13. **The rip back.** Violent — torn and reassembled at once, the hallway
    shattering, dimensions folding, her awareness cramming itself back into
    three dimensions and one temporal. **Back into a body. Back into flesh.**
    Air in lungs she had forgotten. Pavement under her knees, cold and solid.
14. **What he did while she was gone.** Firas inches away, face to face. Sharp
    pain in her abdomen. **The autoinjector against her, his hand on it.** He
    used the dose she was keeping for him. **He looks up at her serene, and
    smiling.**
15. **THE HUMAN CHAIN — where the last names land.** Ruth's hands on the arm
    that has solidified enough to hold, face contorted, screaming something
    lost in the roar. Behind Ruth: **Victor at Ruth's waist, Leah holding
    Victor, BEN anchoring Leah, TESS at the back with her arms around Ben and
    her legs braced on the overturned bus.** All of them hauling her back.
    **They came for her. They refused to let her go, and she did not agree to
    any of it.**
16. **Span 21, then 22.** She lunges at his hands and tries to stand and the
    chain holds her.
17. **STUD 7.** He is still smiling. His lips move and **she cannot hear him
    over the roar, so she reads it**: spans 23–24. Then span 25.
18. **He goes.** The cracks spreading, light consuming him from inside. Pulled
    toward the point — **not dragged; moved, as though space shifted and took
    him with it.** Eyes on her the whole way. **He looks back once at the
    edge.** Then in. Light and energy and everything that had been her brother,
    compressed, absorbed. **Gone.**
19. **The collapse.** The singularity pulses once more and winks out — the red
    finished its work, the essence consumed. Air rushing in to fill the vacuum,
    the pressure change like a wall, debris clattering down. **The bank groans
    and holds.** Silence.
20. **END.** The chain breaks and the team goes down in a heap, gasping,
    alive. Ruth still has her arm and has not let go, pulling her into the pile.
    **Ahdia in the centre of them, surrounded, held.** And the space where
    Firas was is empty — no body, no trace, scorch marks fading. **She was
    saved because he chose it. She did not get to say goodbye.** Stop there.
