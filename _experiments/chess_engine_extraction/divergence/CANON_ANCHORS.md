## What counts as what, when scoring

- **Convergent** — the blind beat lands on substantially the same event *and* the same
  emotional function. Rare, and the interesting kind of rare.
- **Structurally convergent** — same function, different event. The move produced "the
  moment the character's competence stops being enough" in both, via different incidents.
  This is the result that would say the scaffold is doing real work.
- **Divergent** — the move produced something the draft does not contain at all.
- **Contradictory** — the blind beat requires something canon forbids.

The hypothesis worth testing: convergence should cluster where the *board* is loud
(captures, checks, the pawn that never promotes) and divergence should cluster where the
board is quiet, because quiet moves under-determine the beat and personality has to fill
the whole space. If that holds, the engine is doing structural work at the loud moments
and the draft is doing authorial work at the quiet ones.
