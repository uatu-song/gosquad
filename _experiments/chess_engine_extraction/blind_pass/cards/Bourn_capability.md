# Bourn — capability and cost

*Held in a separate file from the personality card deliberately. Nothing about how a capability was acquired is stated anywhere in this packet; do not infer an origin, and do not connect it to anything in the character's history.*

Nothing beyond the institutional and the personal: command of large resources, decades of pattern recognition, and field skills — small arms, close quarters, tradecraft — maintained well past the age most would let them lapse. The body is the limit now: old injuries ache, stamina is not what it was, and she relies on others to do what she once did herself. The deeper cost is charged elsewhere — nightmares, functional drinking, and no one to retire to.
