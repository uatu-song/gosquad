# Victor — capability and cost

*Held in a separate file from the personality card deliberately. Nothing about how a capability was acquired is stated anywhere in this packet; do not infer an origin, and do not connect it to anything in the character's history.*

Nothing beyond the mind and the network: genuine strategic reasoning, pattern recognition across sources, and a neighbourhood that answers his calls. Physically a runner, not a fighter — he tires fast and depends on others for protection. The cost of the mind is that it never switches off, and it is loudest at the hour it is least useful.
