# Firas — capability and cost

*Held in a separate file from the personality card deliberately. Nothing about how a capability was acquired is stated anywhere in this packet; do not infer an origin, and do not connect it to anything in the character's history.*

What he has is expert free running — reading a built environment for lines other people cannot see, vaulting, falling safely, arriving places he should not be able to reach — plus improvised self-defence, protective fighting built around shielding rather than winning, and a real gift for getting strangers to agree to things. The cost is straightforward and unglamorous: a body that is only a body, joints that already ache, and no margin for error whatsoever.
