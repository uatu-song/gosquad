# Ahdia — capability and cost

*Held in a separate file from the personality card deliberately. Nothing about how a capability was acquired is stated anywhere in this packet; do not infer an origin, and do not connect it to anything in the character's history.*

She can change how fast time runs inside a zone around her without changing her own experience of it — hold a thing still, slow it, speed it up — within roughly a city block, best within thirty or forty feet, and only while her concentration holds. She perceives branching outcomes before one of them settles, a permanent low hum of *the version where this goes wrong*. Every use is paid for out of her body: nosebleeds, deep exhaustion, recovery time, cumulative and irreversible. Fear and grief destabilise her control. Some things she cannot touch at all, and she cannot always tell in advance which.
