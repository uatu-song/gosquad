# Ryu — capability and cost

*Held in a separate file from the personality card deliberately. Nothing about how a capability was acquired is stated anywhere in this packet; do not infer an origin, and do not connect it to anything in the character's history.*

What he has is a rare interface talent — he is the one person who can consistently make a difficult class of autonomous systems do what they are supposed to do — plus theoretical physics, real-time tactical coordination, and heads-up display design built out of video game logic because that is the grammar he thinks in. The cost is that all of it is exercised at a distance. He can see everything and reach nothing; his instruments give him the best possible view of harm he cannot intervene in, and that view is the price of the seat.
