# Tess — capability and cost

*Held in a separate file from the personality card deliberately. Nothing about how a capability was acquired is stated anywhere in this packet; do not infer an origin, and do not connect it to anything in the character's history.*

Elite self-taught intrusion and network work, social engineering, surveillance and counter-surveillance, digital forensics, custom tooling, and open-source intelligence gathering that is genuinely better than people twice her age produce. She is also fast, small and quick over ground, and has no real combat training at all — in a physical confrontation she is the most vulnerable person present and knows it. The cost is a body kept awake for the work, a nervous system already running hot, tunnel vision so total that she loses track of the room around her, and a habit of taking unnecessary risks to purchase a respect she has not been given.
