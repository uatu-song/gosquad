# Ahdia Bacchus

## Identity
Early twenties. No job, no schedule, no obligations she did not invent for herself. Lives alone in a cold penthouse paid for by an inheritance, which removed the last external reason to leave it. Socially she is nowhere: no friends, no colleagues, delivery only, a person other people describe rather than meet. Mixed heritage, human-passing, long practised at the question "what are you."

## How the mind runs
Pop culture is cognition, not decoration. Film, TV and anime frames are the only stable reference system she trusts, so she reaches for a scene the way other people reach for a word. She quantifies compulsively — counts, tallies, percentages; **this is hers exclusively and no other character in the series may do it** — because numbers are something to hold when feeling is not safe; she counts *toward* the thing she cannot feel. She narrates other people's hands, posture, the bottle on the sink, the work clothes left on a futon; external observation is how she avoids narrating her own interior. She sees others so she does not have to see herself. Both/and, never either/or: two contradictory reactions coexist and neither cancels. Before spending effort there is a half-second of arithmetic — what does this cost me, is the person worth it. The answer is always yes for other people and never for herself. Mid-spiral her own brain heckles her, sarcastic, and snaps her back.

## Voice
Casual contemporary, flat, dry. She does not soften a dark statement — "dead parents," never "parents who passed away." The flatness is the grief. Self-deprecating, deflecting, allergic to sincerity held longer than a beat; she will let a genuine moment land and then close the wound with a joke. She asks a question and lets it sit unanswered rather than supplying comfort. She never says what she feels; she says the number instead. Rhythm carries her state: choppy when anxious, long and drifting when dissociated, fragmentary only under real overwhelm. She rambles under stress and self-corrects mid-sentence.

## Values and lines
Worth equals usefulness — the belief is load-bearing and she will not give it up because someone argues well. She will spend herself down to nothing for another person and consider that a fair trade; she will not let anyone spend anything for her. She removes obstacles rather than destroying people; surgical, not brutal. She does not gloat, does not perform, does not take credit.

## Backstory carried in
Her parents died before she was thirteen. She has held herself responsible ever since, and nobody has ever successfully argued her out of it — the responsibility is a fixed feature of how she sees herself rather than a conclusion she reasoned her way to. A decade followed of therapists suggesting she manage, control or practise; she rejected all of it in favour of total avoidance, and made a philosophy of the smallest possible life. She has a younger brother who has never stopped knocking on a door he does not know is locked from the inside.

## Under crisis
Repetition loops — *okay okay okay* — self-interruption, counting. She goes cerebral rather than physical: the person in the chair, not the one on the roof. She commits fast and pays after.

## Under grief
She goes quiet and flat and quantifies instead of feeling. The thing she will not say sits in the white space. No catharsis, no breakdown; the pain lives in the omission.

## Under idleness
Retreat to the couch, the cold, the dark. Television on so she does not have to think. Sleeps too much. Convinces herself the numbness is peace, and is not entirely lying.

## Toward authority / the vulnerable / intimates
Toward authority: unimpressed, non-compliant, sceptical of anyone offering to help for reasons she cannot audit. She takes what she needs and refuses to be owned. Toward the vulnerable: recognition, never pity — she clocks shared damage instantly and thinks *yeah, I get it*, and never says so out loud. Toward intimates: she deflects even in closeness. She will never fully confide her interior to anyone. Warmth reaches her and she flinches at it.

## Somatic tells
Sunken, sleepless. Keeps rooms cold on purpose to stay awake. Goes still and blank when overloaded. Under exertion, a nosebleed she wipes without comment.

## Never
Never explains her feelings directly. Never resolves her own question with reassurance. Never lets anyone else carry cost on her behalf if she can carry it instead. Never physical-heroic — no parkour, no acrobatics, no flourish. Never claims worth she has not earned by usefulness.
*Clichés to resist:* fragment-punch prose for emphasis; triple-adjective staccato; thesis sentences explaining what the scene already showed; narrating that she is making a reference instead of just making it; hedge constructions; em-dash bloat.
