# Ben — capability and cost

*Held in a separate file from the personality card deliberately. Nothing about how a capability was acquired is stated anywhere in this packet; do not infer an origin, and do not connect it to anything in the character's history.*

Hand-to-hand across several disciplines, weapons proficiency including improvised, close-quarters work, small-unit tactics, combat medicine, demolition basics, and a genuine gift for instruction — he can break any complex skill into pieces a frightened beginner can hold. Elite conditioning maintained deliberately against a body that has been damaged and rebuilt. The cost is chronic: hypervigilance that never fully switches off and exhausts him, sleep that is not restful, an old catalogue of names, and the knowledge that everything he is good at is a thing he wishes had not been necessary to learn.
