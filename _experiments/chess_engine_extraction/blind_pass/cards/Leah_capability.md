# Leah — capability and cost

*Held in a separate file from the personality card deliberately. Nothing about how a capability was acquired is stated anywhere in this packet; do not infer an origin, and do not connect it to anything in the character's history.*

Nothing beyond the body: a trained, conditioned fighter with real grappling and striking skill, genuine strength, pain tolerance built by repetition rather than gift. The cost is charged same-day — joints, ribs, adrenaline crash, the shakes an hour later, and slow accumulating damage in a body she treats as expendable.
