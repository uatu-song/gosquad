# Ruth — capability and cost

*Held in a separate file from the personality card deliberately. Nothing about how a capability was acquired is stated anywhere in this packet; do not infer an origin, and do not connect it to anything in the character's history.*

Expert trauma medicine performed in bad conditions with improvised tools, rapid triage, pharmacology, and an anatomist's knowledge she can turn to non-lethal stopping power. Intermediate movement training, basic self-defence, high pain tolerance and a genuinely extraordinary tolerance for sleep deprivation. The cost is the double life itself: chronic exhaustion, a hand that is very steady until it is not, and the slow accrual of decisions about who gets helped first that she keeps rather than processes.
