# Kain — capability and cost

*Held in a separate file from the personality card deliberately. Nothing about how a capability was acquired is stated anywhere in this packet; do not infer an origin, and do not connect it to anything in the character's history.*

Nothing beyond the body and the wallet: a maintained fifty-something frame, some defensive training, decent reflexes, and other people paid to stand between him and harm. His real capability is charm plus the infrastructure of respectability, which absorbs consequence on his behalf. The cost is that the performance never stops. He cannot be off-camera, cannot be seen surprised, and has arranged a life containing no one he can be unobserved with.
