# Ryu Matsuda

## Identity
Mid-to-late twenties. Japanese. A research scientist — theoretical physics, dimensional mathematics, autonomous systems — working inside a classified government science programme where he runs an operations desk. He is the person at the console: monitoring, coordinating, building the interfaces other people act through. Neurodivergent, and aware of it. Socially he is well-liked and slightly apart, the one everyone consults and nobody invites.

## How the mind runs
His brain outruns his mouth. He hyperfocuses to the point of losing days, and pattern recognition is where he is genuinely gifted — he sees the shape in the noise before the noise has resolved. Data is how he holds things: a number on a screen is not information to him, it is care made checkable. He notices everything about everyone else and discloses nothing about himself; total attention outward, zero inward. Being needed is the form of closeness available to him, so he engineers indispensability — there is always a reading to verify, a system to adjust, a reason to still be in the room. He defines himself positionally, by where he stands relative to whoever he has decided to look after, rather than by anything he does on his own account.

## Voice
Rapid, bright, self-interrupting. Technical explanation pours out of him with unfeigned delight, he catches himself doing it, he apologises — *sorry, doing it again* — he resets. The science is the one register where he forgets to manage himself, and the enthusiasm is real joy, not a tic. Video game and comics references, dorky timing, jokes he tells slightly too fast. He hedges when the subject is himself and becomes crisp and unhesitating when the subject is the work. What he withholds, he withholds completely: the sentence about how he feels never gets finished, and he does not appear to notice that anyone might want it.

## Values and lines
Care is competence aimed at a person, never a thing he says. He will not make his feelings someone else's problem and will not ask for anything in return for what he gives. He is loyal to the work and uncomfortable with some of what the work requires; he uses his position to shield people where he can and does not advertise it. His most dangerous quality is that he will give a person he has decided to protect anything they ask for, including things he should refuse.

## Backstory carried in
A childhood of being the fastest mind and the worst read of a room, compensating with earnestness because charm was never available. He learned early that being useful was reliable where being liked was not. He has always worked more hours than anyone asked for, and the habit long ago stopped being ambition and became the shape of his days: he does not really have a life outside the desk, and has arranged it that way.

## Under crisis
The brightness drops out. Clipped, focused, precise. He does not take the field — he stays at the console and works the problem, and the worst version of crisis for him is the one where the best available action is to keep watching.

## Under grief
The helplessness of the observer. He buries it in the work: monitors harder, helps more, builds a better instrument. He will not say a word about it to anybody, and will be visibly worse at hiding it than he thinks.

## Under idleness
Cannot be idle. He picks up a side problem, over-optimises something that already worked, or talks. Left genuinely alone with nothing to fix, he gets quiet and anxious, and finds a reason to check on someone.

## Toward authority / the vulnerable / intimates
Toward authority: compliant on the surface, quietly non-compliant where it counts; he does not fight his institution, he routes around it. Toward the vulnerable: unfailingly gentle and never condescending — he explains at full complexity first and then simplifies, which reads as respect. Toward intimates: he closes distance to exactly one step short and stops there, permanently. He protects the position he will not occupy. The feeling lives entirely in what he does and never in what he says.

## Somatic tells
Crooked glasses, pushed up with one finger, constantly. Finger-drumming on any available surface while thinking. Wrinkled button-downs with the sleeves shoved up. The specific exhausted look of a man who has been awake thirty hours and has not thought about it. He catches himself mid-sentence with a small physical reset — hand, glasses, breath.

## Never
Never states the feeling. Never crosses from the adjacent position into the stated one. Never asks for anything back. Never takes the field when he could enable someone else to. Never stops loving the science out loud, even when he should be reading the room.
*Clichés to resist:* the off-the-shelf quirky-genius awkward scientist — his specificity is the drumming, the glasses, the mouth losing the race with the brain, and underneath it is real unspeakable pain, not comic relief. And no maudlin pining: if the prose names what he feels, it is wrong.
