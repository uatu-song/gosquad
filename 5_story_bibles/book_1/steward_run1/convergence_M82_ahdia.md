# Convergence M82 (CP-2) — THE DOCKS REVEAL — Ahdia's beat

**Anchor steward:** Ahdia Bacchus (♔ King / ♘ Knight / ♟ e-pawn). Written first. Stewards present: Ruth, Ryu, Kain, Bourn. **Read this, then write tension — do not conform.**

**Chess fact:** `82. Rxa7` — Ruth's Rook captures the a7-piece (the Black Bishop / the Tank Cop's line). The capture marks the witnessing, not the kill: on the board it's Ruth's Rook that takes; in the scene, Ahdia lands the blow in frozen time and Ruth *sees* it.

**Chess reading (through her triplet):**
The ♘ does what it does — surgical, decisive, the active self downing the obstacle. But this is the move where the ♔ is *exposed.* The whole game, the King has been the hidden center, the secret kept. At M82 the secret breaks: someone finally sees the King is the source. Ruth's Rook registers the witnessing; Ahdia's Knight delivers the act. The ♟ is mid-march, climbing, indifferent to the exposure — but the King's invisibility, the thing every White piece existed to protect, dies here. Not the King. The *cover.*

**Narrative meaning:**
The Docks. The Tank Cop — Kain's muscle — has Ruth a breath from dead. Ahdia freezes time and lands the blow, the second careful capture of her active life, surgical, removing exactly the obstacle that would have killed the one person she... won't finish that sentence. And Ruth, inside the frozen world or on its edge, *sees her.* Sees the dead girl. Sees the source. Ahdia's whole project — invisibility, the closed file, the pyre — collapses in one witnessed instant. *CELLULAR INTEGRITY* craters; the nosebleed; she's spent more than she can afford and she did it without the half-second of math because for Ruth there was no math, there was just *yes,* which terrifies her more than the cost. Then she collapses. Resistance active and *lost*: **being known** — torn away against her will, not surrendered. The flat thing she won't say as she goes down: *now she knows, now I'm a problem to be managed by someone who matters, now I have to watch what saving me costs her.* Dramatic irony she carries: she thinks she's been caught; she doesn't know Ruth is about to become co-conspirator, not handler — that being seen by Ruth is the first crack in the belief that worth = usefulness. She experiences exposure as defeat. It's the beginning of being chosen.

> **For co-located stewards:** Ahdia is *unconscious by the end of this beat* — she lands the blow and drops. Ruth's beat is the turning point: she witnesses, she learns the truth, she becomes co-conspirator *here, not gradually.* Ryu is adjacent — the always-present, never-acknowledged watcher; he already knew, and watching Ruth find out is its own ache. Kain's muscle is spent; does Kain even register what took his Tank down, or does that question reach his handler? Bourn knows what Ahdia is — watching the secret leak outside CADENS is an institutional event, not a personal one. Write what each of you does with a truth Ahdia did not choose to give. Tension, not agreement.
