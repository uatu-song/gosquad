- The **8 studs land in order** (see `BOOK1_MOVE_MAP.md` stud table).

- **Ahdia survives**, pulled back from transcendence; ends ~30% baseline, 18–24 months with treatment; declines the CADENS badge.
- **Ruth discovers the truth at the Docks** (M82) and becomes co-conspirator — not gradually, here.
- **Kain is "destroyed" then clone-survives**; campaign unblemished; ends knowing Ahdia saw Bellatrix, proceeding carefully on his handler's order.
- **The team learns the powers were Ahdia's** (~Ch25); nearly disbands after Firas; chooses to continue.
- **The finish is `136. Ng7`** — a resignation in a lost position, *not* on-board mate. Canonical kill = Bourn's CADENS missile + the singularity Ahdia burns out.

```

        +

        +

        +
[their move list + CPs]    (the ownership table above)
        +
[output format]            beats = chess reading → narrative meaning
```

For convergence stewards, append Ahdia's extracted CP output (`convergence_M{n}_ahdia.md`) once it exists.

## Run order (per manual)

1. **Run Ahdia first** (anchor) — all her moves incl. CP-1/2/3. Extract `convergence_M26_ahdia.md`, `convergence_M82_ahdia.md`, `convergence_M131_ahdia.md`.
2. **Run the other 9 in parallel** — each gets the preamble + their embodiment + move list (+ Ahdia's CP files where shared). They do not see each other's output. Divergence is the goal.
3. **Collect** as `{character}_run1.md`.
4. **Evaluate** — 5-agent pass (Timeline Keeper, Status Tracker, Theme Guardian, Reader Proxy, Pacing Monitor) → Enforcer → Director blocking.
