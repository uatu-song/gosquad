## Why this game fits

A quiet symmetrical opening, a **queen sacrifice at M26** that forces a lopsided position, then ~110 moves of grinding attrition to a quiet knight finish. That *is* Book 1's emotional arc: **win by endurance, not fireworks.** The decisive material event is front-loaded (the queen sac); the rest is outlasting. Ahdia's weapon is the same — she doesn't overpower, she refuses to stop. The long endgame is her baseline grinding down.

Two pawns reach the 6th/5th rank at the very end and **never promote** (`136. Ng7` falls first) — the structural image of arrested transcendence (Ahdia pulled back; Firas's sacrifice spent without itself transforming).
