## Arc Summary

**Start (Month 1):** Conservative true believer; faith in evidence, systems, truth mattering; marriage strained by Sarah's Kain cult membership

**Middle (Months 2-8):** Builds perfect Tank Kain evidence package; documents everything methodically; believes truth will prevail when public

**Evidence Fails (Months 8-10):** Tank Kain evidence public, verified, undeniable—polling recovers within days; Eidolon neutralizes truth with fear

**Climax (Months 11-12):** Leah's recording fails too; military skills save him during manhunt; helps stop coup; watches Kain win anyway

**End (Book 2):** Conservative faith completely shattered; operating without ideology because everything he believed was proven wrong; "We did everything right and still lost"

**Key Limitation:** Conservative faith in systems; belief that evidence/truth inherently matters

**Key Costs:** Faith destroyed; Sarah still in cult; country lost despite perfect execution; must rebuild worldview from scratch
