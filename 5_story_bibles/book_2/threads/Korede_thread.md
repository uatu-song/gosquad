## Arc Summary

**Start (Month 1):** Teen observer; at protest with sister; saved by Ahdia; begins attending parkour academy

**Middle (Months 2-10):** Skills developing at parkour academy; watching sister's harassment escalate; protective but too young to act; observer position frustrating

**Climax (Month 11):** Parkour skills save his life during manhunt; escapes with Tess and Leta; sees Eidolon turn neighbors into hunters; understands systemic threat

**End (Book 2):** Survived with family intact; skills proven; radicalization seeds planted; sets up Book 3-4 transformation after Leta's death

**Key Limitation:** Too young to be in the action; observer position; can't protect sister the way he wants

**Key Costs:** Watched sister harassed for months; saw neighbors turn into hunters; lost faith in system

**FUTURE (Book 3-4):** Leta killed by Officer Webb during manhunt; Korede witnesses sister's murder; Tess reveals Go Squad identity to him in the chaos; radicalized by grief; becomes resistance leader

- Parkour skills from Ahdia's academy

- Watched adults fail to protect sister
