## Arc Summary

**Nature:** Fear-amplification entity; not creating fear but amplifying existing fears to irrational levels; works through psychological manipulation rather than direct action

**Key Operations:**
- **Month 1:** Riot amplification (chaos serves Kain)
- **Month 2:** Assassination exploitation (fear serves "law and order")
- **Month 5:** Mob attack (civilians weaponized against protectors)
- **Months 8-10:** Evidence neutralization (Tank Kain reframed as "controversy")
- **Month 11:** Recording neutralization (Leah's truth → "gold diggers")
- **Month 11:** Citywide manhunt (peak amplification, civilians hunt Go Squad)

**Weakness Revealed (Month 11):**
- Cannot amplify fear of people looking pathetic
- Reality TV format strips mystique from monsters
- Power deflation breaks amplification loop
- Laughter interrupts fear cycle
- FAERIS intervention demonstrates mechanism

**End State (Book 2):**
- Weakened but not destroyed
- Election still delivered for Kain
- Brief window doesn't prevent outcome
- Will recover and continue in Book 3

**Key Mechanism:**
- Works with existing fears, doesn't create new ones
- 60-70% of resistance-trained people can resist
- Binary thinking exploitation (good/bad, safe/dangerous)
- Targets protector-protected relationship specifically
- Makes communities fear their own defenders

**Strategic Importance:**
- Explains how perfect evidence fails
- Explains how truth loses to fear
- Explains Kain's electoral invincibility
- Sets up Book 3-7 resistance practice expansion
