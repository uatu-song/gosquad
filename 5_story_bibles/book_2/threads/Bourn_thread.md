**Start (Month 1):** CADENS Director; institutional authority; assessing Ahdia and Go Squad as potential assets

**Middle (Months 2-8):** Secret ally forming; providing covert intel to Ruth; identifying Titan Strategic conspiracy; building coup evidence

**Crisis (Months 9-10):** Palmer closing in; prepares for extraction; knows career is over; prioritizes mission over safety

**Climax (Month 11-12):** Extracted by Ben and Ruth; provides critical coup intel; coordinates reactor mission from safe house; watches team succeed and election fail

**End (Book 2):** Career sacrificed; coup stopped; election lost; "We stopped the worst—that matters"; living example of institutional defection

**Key Limitation:** Institutional position limits direct action; political enemies; age catching up

**Key Costs:** Career destroyed; CADENS position lost; decades of work ended; but: prevented global coup

**Key Contribution:** Intel that enabled stopping Titan coup; 28 positions stayed empty; evidence for future prosecutions

**Character Growth:** From "necessary evils" pragmatist to active ally willing to sacrifice career for principles; Ahdia reminded her of who she used to be
