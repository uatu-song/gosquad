## Arc Summary

**Campaign Strategy:**
- Month 1: Manufactured riot → "law and order"
- Month 2: Geneva assassination → sympathy + eliminated opponent
- Month 5: Eidolon mob attack → "dangerous vigilantes"
- Months 8-10: Evidence leaks → reframed as "deep state hoax"
- Month 11: Recording release → "gold diggers"
- Month 11: Manhunt → near-victory, interrupted by FAERIS
- Month 12: Election → wins with 312 electoral votes

**Immortality Mechanism:**
- Clone avatar consciousness transfer system
- When primary body dies, consciousness transfers to prepared clone
- Ahdia exiled him Month 4; he melted and respawned next day
- Makes individual targeting (assassination, exile) useless
- Requires institutional/systemic approach to defeat

**TRIOMF Coordination:**
- 28 global positions targeted (Ahdia's Exile Island vacancies)
- Synchronized coup planned for Election Night midnight
- Coordinating through fusion reactor quantum network
- Stopped by Go Squad; Kain wins election anyway

**End State (Book 2):**
- President-elect of the United States
- Coup failed but election won
- Evidence public, doesn't matter
- Fear > truth demonstrated completely
- Go Squad alive but defeated politically

**Key Limitation:** Clone avatars require preparation; quantum coordination vulnerable to physical disruption

**Key Costs:** TRIOMF global coup failed; 28 positions stayed empty; Exile Island exists (embarrassing if public); evidence public (reputation damaged but not destroyed)

**Strategic Importance:**
- Demonstrates CBT failure (perfect execution, Kain still wins)
- Shows fear > truth in electoral politics
- Sets up Books 3-7 authoritarian presidency resistance
- Clone immortality means long-term opposition required
