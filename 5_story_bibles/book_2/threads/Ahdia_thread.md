## Arc Summary

**Start (Month 1):** Depressed hermit with powers, willing to burn lifespan for strangers, isolated but functioning with team

**Middle (Months 2-9):** Secret benevolent dictator operations via Ryu's intel; Exile Island project (28 targets); lies compounding; baseline dropping from 73% to <10%

**Discovery (Month 8):** Ruth finds truth; team confrontation; Both/And complexity; relationship damaged through necessary honesty

**Climax (Months 11-12):** FAERIS saves team via reality TV; fusion reactor sacrifice; election loss despite everything; Prime revelation

**End (Book 2):** Alive at 0.7% baseline; discovered she's fourth iteration; saved millions but lost country; isolation deepening despite team support

**Key Limitation:** TV as only frame of reference; avoidance through isolation; hero complex preventing honest connection

**Key Costs:** Lifespan burned (73% → 0.7%); friendship with Ruth strained; trust of team damaged; learned she's not the original
