## Arc Summary

**Throughout Book 2:** Watching from shadows; observing Ahdia-4 unknowingly repeat patterns; not interfering with events she engineered

**Key Understanding:** This timeline (Ahdia-4's) is the compromise Prime chose after three failed utopia attempts; Kain winning is part of the "lesser evil" calculation; the alternative was humanity not surviving at all

**Reveal (Final Pages):** Appears to Ahdia-4 after election loss; explains she's the original; promises full explanation in Book 3

**Three Abandoned Timelines:**
1. Utopia attempt #1 → collapsed (younger Ahdia left behind)
2. Utopia attempt #2 → collapsed (younger Ahdia left behind)
3. Utopia attempt #3 → collapsed (younger Ahdia left behind)
4. This timeline → the compromise (Kain wins, humanity survives)

**What Prime Knows:**
- Full time travel mechanics from cyclic universe
- Why each previous timeline failed
- What makes this timeline the "lesser evil"
- Three younger versions of herself abandoned in collapsed worlds
- Guilt that drives her barely-maintained human form

**Key Limitation:** Cannot undo choices; cannot save everyone; guilt of abandonment weighs constantly

**Key Costs:** Three abandoned versions of herself; watching failures repeat while knowing she can't prevent them; choosing evil over extinction

**Purpose in Book 2:** Sets up Book 3 revelation; explains why Kain's victory was "acceptable" in cosmic terms; introduces cyclic universe mechanics
