## Arc Summary

**Start (Month 1):** Tactical strategist with organizing background; believes both/and but hasn't taught it systematically; community networks informal

**Middle (Months 2-7):** Formalizes "but vs and" framework; teaches Leah; builds 127 safe house sanctuaries; watches Leah fail to apply lesson on Leta

**Climax (Months 11-12):** Safe house network saves scattered team during manhunt; organizing enables mission success; election fails anyway

**End (Book 2):** Election lost but organizing proved essential; both/and validated—can do the work AND lose AND keep doing the work; co-leadership with Ruth deepening

**Key Limitation:** Can be didactic/preachy; sometimes theory over action; watching Leah fail lesson was painful

**Key Costs:** Election lost despite perfect organizing; Leah's silence on Leta (his student failed to apply lesson); community traumatized by almost hunting Go Squad

**Key Contribution:** 127 safe houses saved team; resistance practice works; organizing IS heroism
