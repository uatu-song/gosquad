# Ruth Investigation Arc - Scene Cards

**Arc:** Ruth discovers Ahdia's secret global ops through mounting evidence
**Chapters:** 3-18 (active investigation), 19-24 (aftermath)
**Emotional Journey:** Accurate Support → Confused → Suspicious → Investigating → Confronting → Co-conspirator → Boundary-Setter

---

## AHDIA'S REAL EMOTIONAL ARC (Critical Context)

Ruth's investigation only makes sense if we understand what's actually happening with Ahdia:

| Chapter | Ahdia's State | Ruth's Read | Accuracy |
|---------|---------------|-------------|----------|
| 2 | Genuine trauma (Geneva dies) | Grief | CORRECT |
| 3 | Genuine depression, hermit regression | Depression | CORRECT |
| 3-4 | TV news loop—atrocities, dictators, suffering | Depression | CORRECT |
| 4-5 | Depression → rage → guilt transformation | Still depression? | STARTING TO MISS |
| 5+ | Secret ops begin (performing grief to cover) | Something's off | INCORRECT |

**The Pivot Point:** Ahdia's grief is REAL until it transforms. She watches TV news about global atrocities—the same hermit behavior from Book 1—but now she's not escaping. She's cataloging. "I couldn't save Geneva. But I could save *them*."

When the ops begin, Ahdia starts PERFORMING the grief she no longer fully feels. That's when Ruth's accurate medical read becomes wrong.

### Scene Card 3B-4A: The TV Loop
**Chapter:** 3 (late) - 4 (early)
**Location:** Ahdia's quarters, glimpsed

**Setup:**
Ruth checks in. Ahdia is watching news. Not movies, not comfort TV—news.

**What Ahdia Sees:**
Dictators. Regimes. Atrocities. Suffering in places she's never been. People she could reach in seconds.

**Ruth's Observation:**
*News instead of comfort shows. That's... different.*

But she rationalizes: *Maybe she needs to feel connected to the world. Some people grieve by watching news.*

**State:** First micro-discrepancy. Ruth notices but dismisses.

**Plants Seed:** The shift from escapism to engagement. Ahdia isn't retreating—she's targeting.

### Scene Card 4B: The Transformation (Ahdia POV Context)
**Chapter:** 4
**Location:** Ahdia's quarters (Ruth not present)

**What's Happening:**
Ahdia alone. News playing. Another report—ethnic cleansing, military junta, mass graves.

**Ahdia's Internal Shift:**
Depression isn't lifting. It's *transforming*.
- Grief for Geneva → guilt ("I couldn't save her")
- Guilt → rage ("I could have saved her if I'd been faster")
- Rage → redirected purpose ("I can't save her. But THEM—")

**The Decision:**
She starts planning. Not healing. Planning.

**Ruth Doesn't See This:** This is the pivot point Ruth will miss.

## RUTH STEWARD CHECKLIST (Apply to Each Scene)

Before writing any Ruth investigation scene:

- [ ] Is she observing first, acting second?
- [ ] Does her medical training inform her observations?
- [ ] Is she asking questions more than making accusations?
- [ ] Are her hands busy during tense moments?
- [ ] Is her concern genuine, not performative?
- [ ] Is she enabling early, setting boundaries late?
- [ ] Does the scene show her exhaustion appropriately for this chapter?
- [ ] Is her love for Ahdia visible even when angry?
