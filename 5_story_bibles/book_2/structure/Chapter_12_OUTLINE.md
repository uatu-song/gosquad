# Chapter 12: "Triage"

**Month:** 5
**Timeline Event:** Multi-site provocateur attack (Part 2) / TRIOMF discovered
**Emotional Arc:** Impossible Choice → Salvage → Honest Reckoning

**Chapter opens:** Ruth's triage call—the climax of the sequence
**Chapter ends:** Team bonded through honest processing of mixed success/failure

**Continues from:** Chapter 11 "Coordinated"

---

## SCENE 1: The Call

**Location:** Ruth's overwatch position / Intercutting to all three sites
**Tone:** Decision made, consequences in motion
**This is the climax of the two-chapter sequence.**

### Touchpoint A
Ruth frozen with impossible choice. All three sites failing. Everyone waiting.

### Beats

1. Ruth's breath. One second of paralysis.
2. Then clarity. **Triage thinking kicks in.**
3. University: Injuries but stable. EMTs can handle.
4. Community center: Victor detained but safe. Violence contained.
5. Riverside: **Active knife. Bleeding victim. Highest danger.**
6. Ruth keys comms: "Battlea, Gloom Girl—leave university. Get to Riverside NOW."
7. Leah: "But we've got injured here—"
8. Ruth: "I know. **We can't save everyone.**"
9. Silence on comms. The weight of that sentence.
10. Tess: "Copy. Moving."
11. Leah hesitating one beat. Then: "Copy."
12. Ruth: "Victor, hold tight. We'll get you after."
13. Victor from custody: "Understood. Do what you need to."
14. Ruth: "Night Knight, reinforcements incoming. Hold position."
15. Ben, pressure on wound: "Copy. Victim stable but needs hospital. Knife still in play."
16. Ruth tracking all movements on her screen.
17. Leah and Tess abandoning university. Running.
18. Injured people behind them. EMTs arriving. Has to be enough.
19. **Ruth made the call. Now she lives with it.**

### Touchpoint B
The triage decision executed. Reinforcements en route to highest danger. University abandoned to EMTs. Victor left detained. Ruth carrying the weight.

## SCENE 2: Riverside Salvage

**Location:** Riverside Park
**Present:** Ben (with victim) → joined by Leah and Tess
**Tone:** Urgent action, team reuniting, the gamble paying off

### Touchpoint A
Ben holding position with bleeding civilian. Reinforcements arriving.

### Beats

1. Ben maintaining pressure on wound. Victim conscious, scared.
2. Crowd chaos around them. Police riot control in progress.
3. Knife provocateur somewhere in the panic. Still armed.
4. Ben scanning but can't leave victim.
5. **Leah and Tess arriving.** Minutes that felt like hours.
6. Tess: "I've got first aid. Go."
7. Seamless handoff—Tess takes over wound pressure.
8. Ben free to operate. Leah beside him.
9. Ruth from overwatch: "Knife provocateur, six o'clock from your position. Moving toward east exit."
10. Ben and Leah spotting target. Pursuit engaging.
11. Provocateur sees them coming. Runs.
12. Leah's speed advantage. Cutting off escape route.
13. Ben flanking from other side.
14. Provocateur cornered. Pulls knife. Threatening.
15. Crowd giving space—fear of weapon.
16. Ruth: "Careful. Non-lethal. We need him talking."
17. Ben and Leah coordinating silently. Practiced teamwork.
18. Ben feinting left. Provocateur tracking him.
19. Leah striking from right. Disarm attempt.
20. Provocateur slashing—but Leah faster. Roller derby reflexes.
21. Dodges blade. Kicks weapon hand.
22. Knife clattering away. Ben tackling provocateur down.
23. Zip-ties. Secure in seconds.
24. Provocateur cursing but contained.
25. Ben checking gear: Earpiece, tactical vest. Same pattern. Hired.
26. Leah recovering knife. Evidence secured.
27. Tess at first aid: "Victim stabilized. EMTs arriving."
28. Ruth: "Riverside secure. Knife down. Victim getting help."
29. Crowd seeing Go Squad save situation. Some cheering.
30. Police arriving but violence de-escalated.
31. **Riverside salvaged from worst case.**

### Touchpoint B
The gamble paid off. Knife secured, victim saved, team reunited. But the cost at other sites still hanging.

## SCENE 3: Victor Extraction

**Location:** Community center → police holding area
**Present:** Team moving to extract Victor
**Tone:** Problem-solving, professional, but aware of failures around them

### Touchpoint A
Victor still detained. Team needs to extract without escalating police conflict.

### Beats

1. Ruth's next call: "Everyone to community center. Victor needs extraction."
2. Ben and Leah leaving Riverside. EMTs handling victim. Police securing scene.
3. Tess joining them. All three heading to Victor's position.
4. Community center violence mostly subsided—burnt itself out.
5. Victor in holding area. Provocateur he caught being processed separately.
6. Police confused: Why did masked vigilante restrain someone?
7. Ruth coordinating: "We need Victor out without escalating."
8. Ben's idea: "Anonymous tip. Show police the provocateur's gear."
9. Tess making call to tip line: Professional equipment. Hired agitator. Check his vest.
10. Police investigating Victor's "arrestee."
11. Discovering earpiece. Tactical equipment. Not a protester.
12. Realization dawning: Victor was stopping the actual problem.
13. Police releasing Victor. Warning only: "Don't interfere with operations."
14. Victor: "I was helping."
15. Officer: "Next time, let us handle it."
16. Victor biting tongue. Not the fight for tonight.
17. Reuniting with team outside community center.
18. Ruth: "Everyone accounted for?" Full team present.
19. But looking around—community center aftermath visible.
20. Broken windows. Scattered debris. People hurt.
21. University the same. Riverside barely salvaged.
22. **They saved some. Lost some.**
23. Ruth: "Extract. Debrief at base."
24. Team withdrawing into night.

### Touchpoint B
Team reunited. Victor free. But the landscape of partial failure surrounding them. No triumphant exit—quiet extraction.

## SCENE 4: Honest Reckoning

**Location:** Parkour Academy back room
**Present:** Full team
**Tone:** Exhausted, honest, processing—not triumphant, not defeated

### Touchpoint A
Team assembled for debrief. Still in tactical gear. Weight of the night visible on everyone.

### Beats

1. Back room. Dim lighting. Team collapsing into chairs.
2. Masks off. Just people now. Exhausted.
3. Silence. No one knows how to start.
4. Ruth should lead debrief but hesitating.
5. **Leah breaks first:** "We left injured people at university. Just... left them."
6. Not accusatory. Just processing. Grief in her voice.
7. Ruth not defensive: "I know. I made that call."
8. Tess: "Six people hurt because we weren't enough."
9. Ben: "Seven. Riverside stabbing victim makes seven."
10. Silence. Weight of casualties settling.
11. Victor: "But Riverside victim would've died if we hadn't committed there."
12. Ben: "Knife provocateur would still be loose. Could've killed more."
13. Leah processing: "So Ruth's call saved lives, even though it cost at university?"
14. Ruth: "I don't know. Maybe. **Both/and.** Saved some, couldn't save all."
15. Tess: "I hated leaving those people. But... Ruth was right about the knife."
16. Victor: "Being detained sucked. But I trusted Ruth to handle it."
17. Ruth acknowledging: "I left you there longer than I wanted. Prioritized the knife."
18. Victor: "You made the triage call. That's leadership."
19. Ben: "Military taught me: Perfect mission planning fails on contact. We adapted."
20. Leah: "We saved the first site completely. That was perfect."
21. Ben: "Then we spread thin. That's on me—I heard multiple sites, pushed to cover them."
22. Ruth: "No. I made the call to split. **I take responsibility.**"
23. Silence. Team processing Ruth's accountability.
24. Victor: "We all chose to go. No one forced us."
25. Tess: "And we saved a lot of people. Not everyone, but a lot."

### Touchpoint B
Honest accounting. No one pretending it was victory. No one wallowing in defeat. The both/and settling in.

## SCENE 5: Learning and Growth

**Location:** Same—debrief continuing
**Tone:** Shifting from processing grief to tactical learning

### Touchpoint A
Team moving from emotional processing to what they learned.

### Beats

1. Ruth: "What did we learn? Tactically."
2. Ben: "Enemy can coordinate multiple simultaneous operations. More organized than expected."
3. Leah: "Same gear, same tactics, same playbook across sites. Training."
4. Victor: "**TRIOMF.** Has to be. Professional provocateurs."
5. Tess: "Why though? What's the goal?"
6. Ben: "Creates footage. Justifies crackdowns. Kain's law-and-order narrative."
7. Ruth: "So this is political. Election strategy."
8. Ben: "Every riot they manufacture is campaign material."
9. Leah: "We stopped it at one site perfectly. Proved it's possible."
10. Victor: "But can't be everywhere. Need different strategy."
11. Ruth: "Alert networks? Warn organizers before protests?"
12. Victor: "I can do that. Community connections."
13. Ben: "I'm tracking TRIOMF operations. More intel = better positioning."
14. Leah: "What about the feeling? Everything felt bigger than it should've been."
15. Tess: "Yes! Like anger was amplified. Fear too strong."
16. Ruth: "I felt it coordinating. Emotions not matching threat level."
17. Ben: "**Eidolon?** Same thing from before? Fear manipulation?"
18. Victor: "If something's pushing emotions artificially, makes our job harder."
19. Ruth: "Something to investigate. But tonight—we adapted, we survived."
20. Ben: "First site crowd cheered when we stopped the provocateurs. They saw us help."
21. Leah: "Riverside crowd cheered too. When the knife went down."
22. Tess: "People know we're trying. Even when we can't save everyone."

### Touchpoint B
Tactical lessons extracted. TRIOMF connection confirmed. Eidolon influence recognized. Team has data for next time.

## SCENE 6: Bonds Strengthening

**Location:** Same—debrief winding down
**Tone:** Quiet solidarity, exhaustion, but unity

### Touchpoint A
Processing complete. Team finding footing together.

### Beats

1. Ruth: "I need to say something."
2. Team looking at her. Waiting.
3. Ruth: "Those calls tonight—leaving university, delaying Victor extraction..."
4. Voice cracking slightly: "I hated making them. Still not sure they were right."
5. Leah: "But you made them anyway. When we were paralyzed, you chose."
6. Victor: "Leadership isn't always making right calls. Sometimes it's just making calls."
7. Ben: "Indecision kills. You kept us moving."
8. Tess: "I trust you, Ruth. Even when your calls hurt."
9. Ruth processing their support. Not expecting this.
10. Victor: "We're not superheroes. Just people with impossible choices."
11. Leah: "Tonight proved we can't save everyone. That sucks. But we saved who we could."
12. Ben: "Seven injuries across four sites is better than dozens dead."
13. Tess: "And we got evidence. Provocateurs caught. Gear documented."
14. Victor: "My community will know agent provocateurs are real now. Police saw the equipment."
15. Ruth: "So we learned, we adapted, we saved lives, we got evidence."
16. Team nodding. Exhausted but united.
17. Ruth: "Then we did our job. Not perfectly. But we did it."
18. Beat of silence. Acknowledgment.
19. Victor: "Go home. Rest. Process. We earned it."
20. Team beginning to stand. Masks off. Just people now.
21. Ruth stopping them: "Wait. Everyone... thank you. For trusting me tonight."
22. Leah: "Thank you for leading. Even when it sucked."
23. Small gestures of solidarity. Nods. Brief touches.
24. Ben leaving first. Needs to process alone.
25. Tess and Leah together. Partners supporting each other.
26. Victor and Ruth last. She needs to debrief more. He knows.
27. **Night ending. Team fractured by failure but bonded through honesty.**

### Touchpoint B
Unity forged through shared trauma. Ruth's leadership confirmed not despite the hard calls but because of them. Team stronger for having faced limits together.

## CHAPTER CLOSE

Ruth and Victor alone in the back room.

Victor: "You made the right calls."

Ruth: "Did I?"

Victor: "You made calls. That's what mattered."

Ruth looking at her hands. The weight still there. But shareable now.

Ruth: "Next time they coordinate, we'll be ready."

Victor: "We will."

Lights off. Night over.

**End chapter.**
