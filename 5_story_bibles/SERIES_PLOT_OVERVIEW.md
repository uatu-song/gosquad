# Go Squad: The Series Arc

*Books 1–8 as currently planned, 2 November 2026. Numbering follows the eight-book scheme: Book 3 is the old 2B, and Book 4 is the old Book 3.*

Ahdia Bacchus is a depressed recluse whose power over time burns her lifespan with every use. Across eight books she tries to save everyone by controlling everything, and the attempt nearly destroys her. The series thesis is "You don't have to be fixed to be worthy." Its engine is a therapy progression. In Books 1–4, the Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) approach fails: effort, evidence, heroism and confidence each fall short in turn. Book 5 introduces Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT): accepting reality and holding contradictions. Books 6–8 show it working.

## Part One: Trying Harder (Books 1–4)

### Book 1 — Avoidance to forced action

A crisis in Caledonia drags Ahdia out of isolation. A Hyper Seed bonds with her during a warehouse attack, and she secretly becomes the source of her friends' "powers." Every use eats her cellular integrity. Ruth's CR-7 treatment buys her eighteen to twenty-four months. At the climax Kain fuses with a Tamois Heart and becomes a kaiju. Ahdia nearly dissolves into a hallway of infinite doors, where she glimpses Bellatrix. Her brother Firas injects her with the last treatment and is pulled into the singularity. He is displaced, not dead, though no one knows it. Kain survives through his clone system. The team knows Ahdia has powers and is dying.

### Book 2 — Sacrifice everything (Months 1–7)

The reader never sees inside Ahdia's head. Her friends read her withdrawal as grief for Firas, and it is also a cover. After a riot over the police killing of Isaiah Bennett, she is secretly running global operations, carried by the FAERIS drones and fed coordinates by Ryu, who falsifies her medical records. The world's dictators are vanishing, and the team shrugs it off as the news going crazy. The team's own investigations converge on TRIOMF, Kain's shell network. They watch Kain die and reappear on television the next day. Leta's online harassment turns into death threats. In the last seventy-nine hours Ahdia collapses. Ruth and Ryu find Exile Island: twenty-eight dictators in a reality show that Ahdia runs. Ruth says they'll deal with it Sunday.

### Book 3 — Evidence against fear (Months 8–12)

The book is built on a 28-move chess game in which the Go Squad plays White and loses. The team learns the truth and Ruth constrains Ahdia, who then breaks the agreement. Ben leaks perfect evidence, and Eidolon, a fear-amplifying entity, flips it so Kain gains twelve points. Officer Webb is cleared again. Leah releases her recording of Kain's abuse, and Eidolon turns the city into a manhunt for six people. Webb kills Leta. Tess brutalizes him and leaves him alive. FAERIS broadcasts Exile Island on its own initiative, and the sight of pathetic dictators breaks Eidolon's grip. Eidolon then meets a grief it cannot turn into fear. Bourn, now an ally inside CADENS, reveals a coup timed for Election Night. FAERIS moves the team to the fusion reactor, and Ahdia's ten-minute freeze takes all 28 nodes offline. It costs her everything but 0.7% of her baseline. Kain wins the presidency anyway, and the Go Squad is branded terrorists. Then Prime appears. She is Ahdia-1, the original, and this world is her fifth attempt. "We saved the world and lost our country."

### Book 4 — Confidence to collapse to redemption

Prime burns out Ahdia's powers to prevent a sixth iteration. She moves onto Ahdia's couch as "Aunt Diana." Kain seizes CADENS and the FAERIS network, and Diana secretly enhances the team in Ahdia's place. Ahdia knows and lets them believe it's her. Bellatrix wages war with clone-avatar armies and an Eidolon given a body. At the midpoint, where the book will likely split into 4A and 4B, Bellatrix forces Ahdia through a rift into The Between. The team scatters into guerrilla cells, and Korede builds a resistance network. In The Between, Ahdia's only company is AR-Ryu, an AI copy of Ryu, until Captain Suzie Rivets finds her. Rivets is a mech pilot from an erased iteration. The climax has two parts. Victor wakes Mother FAERIS, the sentient AI exploited beneath CADENS headquarters, and her swarm turns on the clones and severs Eidolon. Then Ruth and Ryu cross into The Between to bring Ahdia home. AR-Ryu dies mid-sentence: "Tell him I loved—". Ruth and Ahdia save each other, breaking the pattern of Firas's sacrifice. Bellatrix admits she chose wrong. Ahdia ends the book powerless but proven.

## Part Two: Learning to Live Broken (Books 5–8)

### Book 5 — The turning point

Two reveals land close together. Jericho, mourned as a hero, faked his death. He controlled Eidolon all along and is building an empire in The Between. Ben, presumed dead, is alive. The team learns Firas was displaced, not killed, and the search begins. CBT gives way to distress tolerance. The book ends in a stalemate reached through acceptance, not control.

### Book 6 — Radical acceptance

Bellatrix is revealed as Ahdia and Firas's biological mother. She is a Type IV being whose kind eliminated emotion, and she caught love anyway. Ahdia learns she was designed as a singularity for a purpose. A new enemy, the Predecessor, is a species that failed Bellatrix's test and rebuilt itself into machines. It now sabotages humanity's chance. Firas is found but unreachable. The team opens its first negotiation with Bellatrix.

### Book 7 — Both/and

Kain's presidency and his policies have doomed the planet. Earth is declared unsalvageable, and a migration vessel is built. The team rescues Firas, who is himself and someone new. The Predecessor makes its final assault and is defeated. Eidolon, made for terror, chooses not to amplify fear. Ahdia earns her powers back through "micro-cycling," a technique built from cyclic-time physics, rather than being given them. Then comes the catch: the vessel can reach near-light speed but cannot stop. Only the siblings' temporal powers can slow it, and that turns out to be what they were designed for.

### Book 8 — Worth without fixing

People choose for themselves whether to board. Kain's loyalists stay with him. The tyrant is revealed as a withered body in a vat, his avatars a farce, and he is left behind as king of the ash heap he made. During deceleration the field collapses. Firas moves to sacrifice himself again, and Ahdia tries to take his place. Bellatrix pushes them both aside and takes the full load: "This is what love is. I understand now." Diana returns from cosmic space and proves that transcendence did not corrupt her. The final image is New Earth in the viewport. They didn't have to be fixed. They just had to keep choosing.

## Not Yet Settled

- **The plans are uneven.** Books 1–4 reflect rulings through September 2026. The plans for Books 5–8 are January 2026 outlines and have no locked endpoints except Firas's return.
- **Book 4 and Book 5 disagree.** Book 4's plan keeps Ben alive throughout and leaves Jericho ambiguous. Book 5's opening assumes both were presumed dead at the end of Book 4.
- **Several questions are open.** Jericho's fate, how Kain is kept off the vessel, and the series endpoint are not yet decided. The plans for Books 4–7 don't yet show how Kain's policies visibly drive Earth toward collapse.
- **Book 2 waits on two rulings.** One is how strictly Ahdia's POV is withheld. The other is which baseline path the second half follows.
