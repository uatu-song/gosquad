# Book 6: Radical Acceptance

**Status:** Planning
**Emotional Arc:** Understanding reality → Acting anyway
**DBT Focus:** Radical acceptance - accept reality as it is, not as we wish

---

## Overview

Book 6 deepens the DBT approach. Characters learn to accept painful truths AND continue acting.

**Core Practice:** "I accept that this is how things are. Now what do I do?"

### Firas Located

## The Predecessor Appears

New antagonist:
- Previous Type 0 species that failed Bellatrix's test
- Now cyborgs/AI (tried to "fix" themselves, became machines)
- Sabotaging humanity's chance to succeed
- "If we couldn't achieve utopia, no one should get the chance"

**Motivation:**
- Jealousy: Watched other species get tested after their failure
- Bitterness: Broke themselves trying to pass
- Nihilism: If perfection impossible, why let anyone try?

**Methods:**
- Sabotaging Earth's remaining resources
- Interfering with evacuation planning
- Attacking dimensional research (prevent finding Firas)
