# Series Arc Decisions

**Created:** 2026-01-14
**Purpose:** Canonical decisions for the 8-book arc. Supersedes conflicting information in older docs.

---

## Series Structure

**Confirmed:** 8 books (not 7)

**Arc:**
- Books 1-4: CBT approach failing
- Book 5: Turning point (DBT introduction)
- Books 6-8: DBT approach succeeding

---

## Antagonist Resolutions

### Kain (Books 1-5)

**Arc:** Mayor → President → Controls CADENS → King of an ash heap

**The Vat Reveal (Late-game twist):**
- Kain Prime is in a vat, near death, feckless, impotent
- All the "Kain" we've seen are clone avatars, juiced up by Bellatrix
- Clone avatars are healthier versions but ultimately a farce
- The powerful tyrant is a withered husk on life support
- **Thematic:** All that power, all that fear, and he's been nothing the whole time

**Vessel Resolution:**
- Kain plans to board the evacuation vessel
- Go Squad prevents this (how TBD)
- He's left behind as king of an ash heap
- Rules over a dying Earth with no subjects worth ruling
- Poetic justice: Got everything he wanted, it's worth nothing

**Note:** This parallels Bellatrix's arc inversely - she starts as cold observer, becomes human through love. Kain starts as human, becomes empty husk through power.

---

### Jericho (Books 1-8)

**Motivation:** Pure ego. No tragic backstory, no sympathetic point.

**Execution:**
- Hints at motivation without explanation
- Let readers surmise - is it childhood trauma? Philosophical conviction? Just narcissism?
- Never confirmed - the mystery IS the point
- "Eliminate equals" philosophy but WHY is never spelled out
- He's terrifying because there's no handle to grab

**Arc:**
- Books 1-4: Hidden mastermind (controls Eidolon, connects Kain-Bellatrix)
- Book 4: "Dies" heroically, escapes to The Between with Mother FAERIS
- Book 5: REVEALED - team realizes they mourned their enemy
- Books 5-8: Builds empire in The Between, leads Rogue's Gallery

### The Collector

**Decision:** REMOVED from antagonist roster

Reasons:

## Power Mechanics

### Cellular Degradation / Treatment Dependency

**Clarification:** This is NOT deus ex machina

The cell regeneration treatment (CR-7) is established in Book 1 as major plot device:
- Ahdia's powers burn her lifespan
- Treatment extends timeline from weeks to 18-24 months
- Creates dependency on Ruth/CADENS
- Book 2: Dependency becomes critical (global vigilantism)
- Books 3-4: Powers blocked/critical baseline

**Power restoration arc:**
- Not sudden fix
- Treatment dependency continues
- "Micro-cycling" (if used) builds on established mechanics
- Powers returning = continued cost, continued dependency

## Vessel / Migration

### Who Gets on the Vessel?

**Self-selection by ideology:**
- Anyone with a brain who isn't a metaphorical nazi/Trump supporter
- Not because they're BARRED - because they follow their leader to their own peril
- Kain supporters stay with Kain (who fails to board)
- Some see the error in their ways (redemption for individuals)
- Poetic: Authoritarianism kills itself through loyalty

**Go Squad:**
- All essential personnel, on vessel
- Ruth: Medical lead
- Ryu: Technical systems
- Tess: Communications
- Korede: Logistics (former resistance leader)
- Ben/Victor/Leah: Security, community organization
